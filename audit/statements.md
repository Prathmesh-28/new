## Financial Statements (`/statements`) — 22 tools

Generates a full suite of accounting-standard-compliant statements and management reports derived live from the firm's bank transactions, loans, payroll, fixed-asset register and confirmed investments. Stakeholders: finance, CA, owner. Backend: every figure is computed in-browser from the live `store` via the finance lib (`incomeStatement`, `balanceSheet`, `cashFlowStatement`, `monthlyCashFlow`, `monthlyAggregates`) and the depreciation lib (`bookValue`, `depreciationBetween`, `accumulatedDepreciation`, `totalGrossCost/NetBookValue/AccumulatedDepreciation`); no statement is persisted — only tool-specific assumption inputs persist via `useFeatureState` into `store.featureData` (the synced "app" KV namespace).

Tabs are driven by the `TABS` array (22 entries). A period preset selector (`month`/`quarter`/`fy`/`ttm`, Indian FY 1 Apr–31 Mar) drives the Income Statement and Cash Flow tabs; all other date-bounded tabs use the same `range` or recompute their own FY/YoY bounds. PDF/Excel export (via `exportPdf`/`exportExcel`) is shown on every tab except Fixed Assets, plus tab-local PDF buttons on Schedule III, Depreciation Schedule and MIS Pack.

- **Income Statement** — inputs: revenue/expense/payroll/COGS transactions, fixed-asset register (depreciation), active loans (interest), period preset → computation: P&L from `incomeStatement` (Revenue → COGS → Gross Profit/margin → payroll + other opex → EBITDA/margin → depreciation → EBIT → finance cost → PBT → income tax estimated at 25% of PBT → Net Profit/margin); side margin cards (Gross ≥30%, EBITDA ≥15%, Net ≥8% health colouring) → output: vertical P&L with % of revenue + PDF/Excel. _Persist: live store (depreciation est. ₹0 until assets added)._ _Class: Backend._
- **Balance Sheet** — inputs: cash/AR/inventory/payables/GST/debt balances + paid-in capital, as-of `today` → computation: `balanceSheet` (current assets, net fixed assets [est. if no register], total assets; current + non-current liabilities; equity with retained earnings as the **balancing figure**); shows a Balanced / Out-of-balance check (Assets = Liabilities + Equity) → output: two-column Assets vs Liabilities & Equity + PDF/Excel. _Persist: live store._ _Class: Backend (retained earnings is a derived balancing plug)._
- **Cash Flow** — inputs: actual bank cash movements over the period + `monthlyCashFlow(12)` → computation: `cashFlowStatement` direct method (receipts − supplier/employee/tax payments = operating; investing with capex shown as "not tracked yet"; financing = loan proceeds − repayments + equity; net change; opening/closing cash) plus a rolling 12-month month-by-month table anchored to today's bank position → output: direct-method statement, opening/net/closing cards, monthly grid + PDF/Excel (adds monthly sheet). _Persist: live store._ _Class: Backend (capex not tracked → investing = 0)._
- **Fixed Assets** (`FixedAssetRegister`) — inputs: user-entered assets (name, category, cost, purchase date, method WDV/SLM, useful life, salvage, optional disposal date), gated by `canEdit()` → computation: per-asset `accumulatedDepreciation`/`bookValue` at today + register totals (`totalGrossCost`/`AccumulatedDepreciation`/`NetBookValue`); WDV rate from `wdvAnnualRate` (Schedule II); add/edit/delete via `addFixedAsset`/`updateFixedAsset`/`deleteFixedAsset` → output: gross-block/accum-dep/NBV summary + register table; feeds real depreciation into P&L and net fixed assets into the balance sheet. _Persist: live store (`store.fixedAssets`)._ _Class: Backend._
- **AS-3 Cash Flow** (`As3CashFlowStatement`) — inputs: period range, direct/indirect method toggle, `cashFlowStatement` + `incomeStatement` → computation: AS-3 / Ind AS 7 format with sections A/B/C; indirect method reconciles PBT + depreciation add-back + finance cost = operating profit before WC, then a single **derived working-capital-&-other plug** (`operating − (netProfit+dep+interest+tax)`) − tax to tie back to the direct operating figure; reconciliation-check card (closing − opening − net < ₹1) → output: statutory cash-flow statement + operating-cash and reconciliation cards. _Persist: live store._ _Class: Backend (indirect WC line is a reconciling plug)._
- **Schedule III** (`ScheduleThreeBalanceSheet`) — inputs: `balanceSheet` at today → computation: Companies Act 2013 Schedule III Part I vertical format — I. Equity & Liabilities (Shareholders' funds: share capital + reserves; Non-current: long-term borrowings; Current: short-term borrowings, trade payables, other CL, GST provisions) and II. Assets (Non-current PPE; Current: inventories, receivables, cash) with Note references (1–8) mapping to the Notes tab; statutory balance check → output: two-section statutory balance sheet + dedicated PDF export. _Persist: live store._ _Class: Backend._
- **Notes to Accounts** (`NotesToAccounts`) — inputs: `balanceSheet` (as-of) + `incomeStatement` (period) → computation: auto-drafts Note A significant accounting policies (5 static policy statements) plus 10 numbered disclosure notes (Share Capital, Reserves & Surplus, Borrowings, Trade Payables, PPE, Inventories, Trade Receivables, Cash, Revenue, Cost/Employee Benefits) reconciling to Schedule III note numbers → output: policy list + two-column note cards; flags that contingent liabilities, related-party, MSME §43B(h), CSR and OCI must be added manually. _Persist: live store._ _Class: Backend (auto-drafted disclosure; statutory items absent)._
- **Comparative** (`ComparativeStatements`) — inputs: `incomeStatement` for current FY-to-date vs full prior comparable FY, view toggle (comparative/common-size) → computation: per-line YoY (`(cur−prior)/|prior|`, with 0-base guard) for 11 P&L lines, or common-size each line as % of revenue (one decimal) for both years → output: comparative table (prior, current, change, YoY%) or common-size table (₹ + %rev for each year). _Persist: live store._ _Class: Backend (comparative/common-size analysis)._
- **Segment Report** (`SegmentReporting`) — inputs: live transactions in window (excl. transfers) → computation: AS-17 / Ind AS 108 proxy — revenue grouped by `counterparty` (revenue category), top 5 segments + folded "Other segments"; total direct cost (expense + payroll) **allocated pro-rata to segment revenue share**; per-segment result and result margin; totals row → output: segment KPI cards + per-segment table (revenue, % of total, allocated cost, result, margin). _Persist: live store._ _Class: Indicative (segments inferred from top counterparties; common costs pro-rata — refine with CA)._
- **Ratio Pack** (`RatioPack`) — inputs: `balanceSheet` (today) + `incomeStatement` (FY-to-date) → computation: Schedule III mandatory + key ratios — Current, Quick, Debt–Equity, DSCR (EBITDA ÷ (interest+ST debt)), Interest Coverage, Inventory/Debtors/Creditors/Net-Capital Turnover, Net Profit, ROCE, ROE; each with formula, benchmark and pass/neutral health flag; turnover ratios use **period-end balances as a proxy for averages** → output: 12 ratio cards. _Persist: live store._ _Class: Indicative (period-end balances proxy averages; cash-centric not ledger-based)._
- **Fund Flow** (`FundFlowStatement`) — inputs: `balanceSheet` at today vs same date one year prior + `incomeStatement` (FY) → computation: lender-format sources & applications — sources = funds from operations (net profit + depreciation), fresh capital, long-term borrowings raised, fixed-asset sales (derived from NBV/dep deltas); applications = capex additions, long-term repayments; closing **balancing figure = change in working capital** → output: two-column sources/applications + WC-movement banner. _Persist: live store._ _Class: Indicative (non-current deltas derived from YoY balance-sheet reconstruction)._
- **Working Capital** (`WorkingCapitalStatement`) — inputs: `balanceSheet` today vs one year prior → computation: line-by-line change in current assets (cash, receivables, inventory) and current liabilities (payables, GST, ST debt, other CL); rule: a rise in a current asset increases WC, a rise in a current liability decreases it; net WC and increase/decrease total → output: schedule of changes in working capital table. _Persist: live store._ _Class: Indicative (prior-period figures reconstructed from balance-sheet logic dated a year back)._
- **Changes in Equity** (`ChangesInEquity`) — inputs: `balanceSheet` today vs one year prior + `incomeStatement` (FY) → computation: SOCIE (Ind AS Division II, Schedule III) — opening capital/reserves from prior BS; movements = profit for period → reserves, capital issued (Δ paid-in capital), dividends/other adjustments as a **balancing reserve-move plug** (`Δretained − netProfit`); closing must tie to current BS equity (tolerance < ₹2 check) → output: capital/reserves/total-equity movement table + tie-out banner. _Persist: live store._ _Class: Backend (dividends/other line is a balancing plug; OCI absent)._
- **Depreciation Sch.** (`DepreciationSchedule`) — inputs: `store.fixedAssets`, FY bounds → computation: Companies Act Schedule II per-asset opening/closing WDV (`bookValue` at FY start/end), depreciation for year (`depreciationBetween`, telescopes exactly), accumulated dep, method (WDV/SLM), useful life, disposal flag; register-level totals (gross cost, accum dep, dep-for-year, NBV) → output: KPI cards + per-asset schedule with totals + dedicated PDF; empty state prompts adding assets. _Persist: live store (`store.fixedAssets`)._ _Class: Backend._
- **EPS & Net Worth** (`EpsNetWorth`) — inputs: `balanceSheet` (as-of) + `incomeStatement` (period) + user assumptions face value (default ₹10) and potential dilutive shares → computation: shares = paid-up capital ÷ face value (proxy); Basic EPS = net profit ÷ shares; Diluted EPS = net profit ÷ (shares+potential); AS-20 / Ind AS 33; Net Worth (Companies Act §2(57)) = paid-up capital + free reserves − accumulated losses; book value per share → output: 4 KPI cards + EPS build-up and Net Worth schedules. _Persist: `stm-eps-face-value`, `stm-eps-potential-shares` (featureData KV)._ _Class: Backend (computation) + KV (assumptions); share count is a proxy (no share register)._
- **Cost Sheet** (`CostSheet`) — inputs: `incomeStatement` (period) → computation: classical cost build-up — prime cost (COGS) + direct labour (payroll) = works/factory cost + depreciation = cost of production + admin/selling overheads (other opex) + finance cost = cost of sales; profit = revenue − cost of sales; each line as % of revenue; margin cards (gross ≥30%, prime ≤60%, overhead ≤25%) → output: cost build-up statement + margin cards. _Persist: live store._ _Class: Indicative (payroll classified as direct labour, opex as overheads — working classification for a cash-centric system)._
- **MIS Pack** (`MisPack`) — inputs: `incomeStatement` + `balanceSheet` + `cashFlowStatement` + `monthlyCashFlow(6)` → computation: board-ready snapshot — 8 headline KPIs (revenue, net profit, net/EBITDA margin, cash, operating CF, receivables, current ratio with health tones), P&L summary, balance-sheet summary (incl. net working capital), 6-month cash trend → output: KPI grid + summaries + cash-trend table + dedicated MIS PDF (KPIs, P&L, BS). _Persist: live store._ _Class: Backend (assembles same live figures as other tabs)._
- **Indirect Cash Flow** (`IndirectCashFlow`) — inputs: `balanceSheet` today vs one year prior + `incomeStatement` + `cashFlowStatement` (FY) → computation: full indirect method building each working-capital movement from YoY balance-sheet deltas (Δreceivables/inventory as use of cash, Δpayables/GST/other CL as source), operating profit before WC = PBT + dep + interest, then − tax = derived operating cash; compares derived vs direct-method operating cash with the difference shown as accrual-timing → output: operating-section build-up + comparison cards. _Persist: live store._ _Class: Backend (decomposed WC, not a single plug; difference is accrual timing)._
- **3-Statement Forecast** (`ProjectedStatements`) — inputs: `incomeStatement` (period run-rate) + `balanceSheet` (today) + user assumptions revenue growth %, cost growth %, horizon months (1–36) → computation: annualises the period window to a full-year base, applies growth across horizon fraction to produce projected P&L (revenue, COGS, payroll, opex, EBITDA, dep, interest, PBT, tax est. 25%, net profit); projected position assumes profit + depreciation accrete to cash with no new capex/financing/dividends (projected cash/equity/assets) → output: KPI cards + projected P&L and projected position schedules. _Persist: `stm-proj-rev-growth`, `stm-proj-cost-growth`, `stm-proj-horizon-months` (featureData KV)._ _Class: Preview (planning model, not a statutory statement) + KV (assumptions)._
- **Break-even** (`BreakEvenAnalysis`) — inputs: `incomeStatement` (period) + user fixed-opex-share % slider (default 70%) → computation: splits costs (COGS fully variable; payroll, depreciation, finance cost fixed; other opex split by slider); contribution = revenue − variable; CM ratio; break-even revenue = fixed ÷ CM ratio; margin of safety (₹ and %); degree of operating leverage (contribution ÷ EBIT) → output: KPI cards (break-even revenue, CM%, MoS%, DOL) + cost-behaviour and break-even/safety schedules. _Persist: `stm-be-fixed-opex-pct` (featureData KV)._ _Class: Backend (computation) + KV (fixed/variable split assumption)._
- **Budget Variance** (`BudgetVariance`) — inputs: user-entered budget targets (revenue, COGS, payroll, other opex) + actuals from `incomeStatement` → computation: per-line variance (actual − budget), variance % (vs budget base), direction-aware favourable/adverse flag (revenue favourable if above, costs favourable if below); EBITDA variance = net effect across all four lines → output: empty-state prompt until a budget is entered, then budget-vs-actual table with status badges and EBITDA total row. _Persist: `stm-budget-targets` (featureData KV)._ _Class: Backend (variance computation) + KV (budget targets)._
- **Monthly Trend** (`MonthlyTrendPL`) — inputs: `monthlyAggregates(transactions, 12, today)` → computation: per-month revenue, expense, cash-basis net (revenue − total expense), margin %; stats (12-month revenue/net, avg monthly revenue, profitable-month count, peak month); revenue bar relative to max → output: KPI cards + month-by-month trend table with revenue trend bars and totals row. _Persist: live store._ _Class: Backend (simplified cash-basis P&L trend — differs from statutory net profit which charges dep/interest/tax)._
