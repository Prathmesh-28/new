# Spend Management (`/spend`) — 18 tools

Surfaces where the money goes and where it's leaking — duplicate vendors, silently-growing subscriptions, category mix vs the tenant's own norm, plus budgeting, policy and approval workflows. Stakeholders: finance, ops. Backend: KV-synced (reads `store.transactions`; user-built records persisted via `useFeatureState` → `store.featureData`, written to localStorage + debounced backend push + cross-device sync).

## Page-level (top, always rendered)

- **Summary cards (4)** — `store.transactions` (negative-amount = expenses) → this-month total spend, count of duplicate-vendor categories, count of growing subscriptions, count of categories >5% over benchmark → four KPI tiles. _Persist: live store._ _Class: Backend._
- **3-Month Spend Trend** — expenses grouped by calendar-month windows (current + prior 2) → summed monthly totals → 3-bar chart (latest bar highlighted red). _Persist: live store._ _Class: Backend._
- **Duplicate Vendor Detection** — expenses grouped by category; categories holding >1 distinct counterparty flagged → top 5 by total category spend, names the highest-spend vendor as consolidation target, shows total spend per category → consolidation-opportunity list. _Persist: live store._ _Class: Backend._
- **Silently Growing Subscriptions** — vendors present in BOTH current month and 3-months-ago window → growth% = (m0−m3)/m3; keep >15% growth & m0>0, top 6 by growth → rising-vendor list (was→now, +%). _Persist: live store._ _Class: Backend._
- **Category vs Your 12-Month Norm (benchmark)** — per-category current-month % of total spend vs the tenant's OWN trailing-12-month median %-mix (median of monthly category shares via `percentiles`); falls back to a static Indian-SMB REFERENCE table only when no history → delta (over >+5% / under <−5%), bar with benchmark marker, source tag (your median vs ref). _Persist: live store._ _Class: Backend (own-norm); Indicative (static reference fallback)._

## Single-scroll sections (each a self-contained component)

- **#94 Spend Categorisation & Top Vendors** — all-history expenses → spend by category (% bars) and top 8 vendors by amount, with top-5 vendor concentration share (>60% = "concentrated" flag) → mix + vendor table. _Persist: live store._ _Class: Backend._
- **#95 Corporate Card / Petty-Cash Manager** — manual rows (holder, card/petty kind, limit/float, spent, note) → totals (limit, spent), per-account utilisation %, count over sanctioned limit → reconciliation list. _Persist: `spend-card-entries` (featureData)._ _Class: KV._
- **#96 Subscription / SaaS Spend Tracker** — manual subs (name, amount, monthly/quarterly/annual cycle, renewal date) → normalise each to monthly run-rate, annualise (×12), flag renewals due within 30 days → KPIs + upcoming-renewal alerts + list. _Persist: `spend-subscriptions` (featureData)._ _Class: KV._
- **#97 Budget vs Actual by Cost Center** — manual cost centers (name, monthly budget, matched spend category) vs this calendar-month actuals pulled live from transactions by category → variance, used%, over-budget flag + count, progress bars. _Persist: `spend-cost-centers` (featureData); actuals from live store._ _Class: KV + Backend._
- **#98 Duplicate / Anomaly Payment Detector** — live expenses → (a) duplicate suspects: same payee + same rounded amount with ≥1 pair within 5 calendar days; (b) outliers: payments > mean + 2.5σ of all spend (needs ≥4 txns) → two lists with counts. _Persist: live store._ _Class: Backend (heuristic)._
- **Spend-by-Category Trend (12 months)** — expenses bucketed into 12 monthly bins per category → 12-bar sparkline each, total per category, rising/easing tag (last-6-mo sum vs prior-6-mo sum) → per-category trend. _Persist: live store._ _Class: Backend._
- **Recurring-Spend Detector** — vendors billed in ≥3 of the last 6 months → avg monthly spend, annualised (×12), months-seen, last-seen; top 10 → auto-detected subscriptions + total annual commitment. _Persist: live store._ _Class: Backend._
- **Budget-vs-Spend Gauge** — manual single business-wide monthly budget vs live month-to-date spend → used%, run-rate projection (MTD/dayOfMonth × daysInMonth), remaining, over / projected-over flags → envelope gauge. _Persist: `spd-monthly-budget` (featureData); spend from live store._ _Class: KV + Backend._
- **Spend Forecast (next 3 months)** — 6-month spend history → avg of non-zero months + simple slope (last-2-mo avg vs prior-2-mo avg) projected forward 3 months (floored at 0) → actual+forecast bar chart, avg monthly, projected next-3-month total. _Persist: live store._ _Class: Indicative (directional projection)._
- **Savings-Opportunity Finder** — live expenses → (a) categories with ≥3 vendors → ~5% consolidation saving; (b) recurring vendors whose 2nd-half-of-6-months spend >1.25× 1st-half → ~50% of the increase as saving; top 8 by saving → estimated annual savings list. _Persist: live store._ _Class: Indicative (heuristic saving estimates)._
- **Expense-Policy Threshold Checker** — manual per-category caps vs this month's live transactions exceeding their cap → breach list (payee, date, cap, amount), top 12 by amount + breach count. _Persist: `spd-policy-rules` (featureData); txns from live store._ _Class: KV + Backend._
- **T&E (Travel) Spend Tracker** — live expenses matched against travel/flight/hotel/cab/fuel keyword list (counterparty + description) → 6-month monthly travel-spend bars, top 6 travel vendors, total + txn count. _Persist: live store._ _Class: Backend (keyword heuristic)._
- **Spend Approval Queue** — manual pre-spend requests (vendor/purpose, amount, requester, note) with pending/approved/rejected status → pending-value total, status filter, approve/reject actions with full status trail. _Persist: `spd-approval-queue` (featureData)._ _Class: KV._
- **Spend Variance vs Last Month** — live expenses for current vs previous calendar month, by category → per-category delta (₹ and %), ranked by largest rupee swing; totals (this/last month, change) → variance list. _Persist: live store._ _Class: Backend._
- **Discretionary vs Committed Spend** — this month's live spend split: committed = payroll/loan/tax, discretionary = ops/transfers → amounts + % of spend, split bar → two-way breakdown. _Persist: live store._ _Class: Backend._
- **Top-Growing Expense Categories** — last-3-months vs prior-3-months spend per category → keep positive deltas, top 6 by rupee increase → ranked rising-cost-head list (₹ delta, % , window-over-window). _Persist: live store._ _Class: Backend._
- **GST / ITC-Eligible Spend Split** — this month's live spend split (ops = ITC-eligible, others = not) + user-set assumed GST rate (clamped 0–28%) → embedded input GST = eligible × rate/(100+rate), eligible %, eligible/ineligible/claimable tiles → ITC planning estimate. _Persist: `spd-itc-rate` (featureData); spend from live store._ _Class: KV + Indicative (tax estimate, not a filing)._
- **Approval Turnaround Tracker** — reads the same `spd-approval-queue`; user stamps decision time per decided request → per-request turnaround (days = decidedAt − created), avg decision time, pending count, oldest pending → SLA view. _Persist: `spd-approval-queue` (read) + `spd-approval-decided` (featureData, decision timestamps)._ _Class: KV._
