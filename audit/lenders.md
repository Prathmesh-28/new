## Lenders (`/lenders`) — 18 tools

Role-gated lender/credit cockpit: AA-verified borrower marketplace plus a borrower-side toolkit for shortlisting lenders, comparing offers, tracking covenants/borrowing-base/disbursement, and running the live debt book. Stakeholders: owner, finance, investor (route guard: only `investor` + `super_admin`; others redirected to `/dashboard`). Backend: `/api/lenders` (queue, bid) + KV via `useFeatureState`; analytical tools derive from the live synced store (`useApp`).

Most tools share `useLenderFinancials()` — a memoized snapshot over `store.transactions/invoices/inventory/bankAccounts/activeLoans` computing revenue, expenses, EBITDA, interest, cash, debt service, debt outstanding, AR (open/overdue), stock at cost, and ratios (DSCR, current ratio, leverage = debt/EBITDA, interest coverage). Ratios sentinel to `99` ("n/a") when no debt/denominator exists.

- **Marketplace (Co-lending Auction)** — fetches AA-verified borrower applications `GET /api/lenders/queue` → shows 3 KPIs (in-queue count, AA-verified count, total loan volume) and per-app cards (business, city, industry, monthly revenue, color-coded credit score, requested date, loan amount); each app opens **BidModal** → lender enters interest rate (8–36% p.a.) + processing fee (0–5%), previews estimated monthly interest income (`loan_amount × rate/100 / 12`), posts `POST /api/lenders/bid {application_id, interest_rate, processing_fee}` → bid placed, local Set marks app as bid (in-session). _Persist: live backend (queue + bid); bid-placed flag in-memory only._ _Class: Backend._
- **Covenant Dashboard** — user-defined covenants (metric ∈ DSCR/current ratio/leverage/interest cover, op min `≥` or max `≤`, threshold) tested live against the financial snapshot → per-row actual, breach test, headroom % ((actual−threshold)/threshold for min; inverse for max), status (breach / at-risk if headroom <10% / ok); KPI counts tracked/at-risk/in-breach; add/delete covenants. _Persist: KV `lender-covenants`._ _Class: KV + Indicative (ratios from live store)._
- **Borrowing-Base Certificate Generator** — inputs AR advance %, stock advance %, exclude-overdue toggle, sanctioned limit, currently-drawn → eligible AR (open less overdue) × AR rate + stock-at-cost × stock rate = gross drawing power; capped to limit; less drawn = available headroom or excess-overdrawn (with margin-call warning); renders a dated borrowing-base certificate. _Persist: none (component state; AR/stock from live store)._ _Class: Indicative._
- **Lender MIS Pack** — period (month) + cadence selectors → assembles a 3-section reporting pack: period-scoped P&L (revenue/opex/net profit from transactions in selected month), live covenant ratios, receivables & liquidity (AR, overdue, inventory, cash, debt outstanding); "Copy MIS pack" copies plain-text to clipboard. _Persist: KV `lender-mis-cadence` (cadence only); period/figures live/in-memory._ _Class: KV + Indicative._
- **Lender Directory & Shortlist** — seeded shortlist of lenders (name, type Bank/NBFC/Fintech, indicative rate, max ticket, turnaround days, 1–5 relationship stars) → blended fit score 0–100 = rate (50%, normalized cheapest-best) + TAT (30%, normalized fastest-best) + relationship (20%), sorted, top flagged with star; editable relationship rating; add/delete lenders. _Persist: KV `lnd-shortlist`._ _Class: KV + Indicative (scoring)._
- **Loan-Offer Comparison** — competing offers (lender, amount, rate, tenure months, fee %) → per-offer reducing-balance EMI, total interest, processing fee, total cost, and effective annual cost (= total cost ÷ principal annualized over tenure); lowest effective-cost offer flagged/starred with summary banner; add/delete offers. _Persist: none (component state)._ _Class: Indicative._
- **Application-Status Tracker** — tracked applications (lender, amount, stage ∈ submitted/docs/underwriting/sanctioned/disbursed/declined, applied-on) → KPI counts (total, in-progress, sanctioned value) and per-app funnel progress bar by stage order; stage dropdown advances/declines; add/delete. _Persist: KV `lnd-app-tracker`._ _Class: KV._
- **Disbursement / Drawdown Planner** — sanctioned limit + tranches (date, amount, note) sorted by date → running cumulative utilization and undrawn balance per tranche; KPIs sanctioned/total-drawn/undrawn; over-sanction warning when draws exceed limit; add/delete tranches. _Persist: KV `lnd-disbursement` (tranches); sanction in component state._ _Class: KV._
- **Rate-Negotiation Prep** — current rate (defaults to outstanding-weighted average of live `activeLoans`) → six live "leverage points" (DSCR ≥1.5×, leverage <2.5×, current ratio ≥1.5×, interest cover ≥3×, overdue AR <10%, cash > annual debt service), each worth fixed basis points (50/50/25/25/25/25) → arguing room (bps), target rate (`max(8, current − bps/100)`), estimated annual saving on outstanding; "Copy negotiation brief" to clipboard. _Persist: none (live store + component state)._ _Class: Indicative._
- **Repayment Track-Record** — per live active loan computes repaid principal (principal − outstanding), % repaid, months elapsed (inferred from amortized principal ÷ EMI, capped at term), months left, next EMI date → KPI totals (borrowed/repaid/outstanding) and per-loan progress cards. _Persist: none (read-only from live `activeLoans`)._ _Class: Indicative._
- **Syndication / Multi-Lender Split** — total facility + participants (lender, share %, rate) → per-participant amount (facility × share%), share-weighted blended rate, total share allocated; KPIs participants/share-allocated/blended-rate; warning if shares ≠ 100% (under/over-allocated); add/delete participants. _Persist: KV `lnd-syndication` (participants); facility in component state._ _Class: KV + Indicative._
- **Security / Collateral Register** — secured exposure + collateral items (asset, type, value, haircut %) → per-item realisable value (value × (1−haircut)), gross security value, total realisable cover, cover ratio (realisable ÷ exposure), shortfall; KPIs gross/realisable/cover-ratio; top-up-demand warning on shortfall; add/delete items. _Persist: KV `lnd-collateral-register` (items); exposure in component state._ _Class: KV + Indicative._
- **Refinance-Opportunity Scanner** — benchmark market rate vs live `activeLoans` → per-loan rate gap (rate − benchmark) and first-year estimated annual saving (outstanding × gap%) for loans >0.5% above benchmark, sorted by saving; flags REFINANCE vs AT-MARKET; KPIs scanned/candidates/total-saving. _Persist: none (read-only from live store; benchmark in component state)._ _Class: Indicative._
- **Lender-Relationship CRM** — seeded lender contacts (lender, contact/RM, last-contacted, next action, next date) → flags overdue (next date < today) / due-today, sorted by next date; KPIs relationships/due-today/overdue; "Log touch" stamps today, add/delete relationships. _Persist: KV `lnd-relationship-crm`._ _Class: KV._
- **Drawdown-vs-Sanction Utilization Trend** — monthly data points (month, sanctioned, drawn) → utilization % per month, sorted; KPIs latest/average/peak utilization; bar chart (amber >75%, red >90%) + table; add/update (re-adding a month overwrites it), delete. _Persist: KV `lnd-utilization-trend`._ _Class: KV._
- **Lender Concentration Risk** — groups live `activeLoans` outstanding by lender → per-lender share, sorted; Herfindahl-style HHI (Σ share²) → concentration level (high ≥0.50, moderate 0.25–0.50, low diversified); KPIs lenders/largest-share/concentration; share table with bars and total; high-concentration warning naming the top lender. _Persist: none (read-only from live store)._ _Class: Indicative._
- **Interest-Paid Summary** — per live active loan estimates annual interest (outstanding × rate) and monthly interest, sorted by annual interest → KPIs total annual/monthly interest, blended cost of debt (total annual ÷ total outstanding); per-loan table with totals row; rates ≥16% flagged red. _Persist: none (read-only from live store)._ _Class: Indicative._
- **Sanction vs Drawn / Repaid** — per live active loan compares sanctioned principal vs current outstanding → repaid (principal − outstanding) and repaid %; KPIs total sanctioned/outstanding/repaid; per-loan progress bars with totals row. _Persist: none (read-only from live store)._ _Class: Indicative._
