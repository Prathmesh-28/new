## CFO Brief (`/cfo-brief`) — 19 tools

A view-switched CFO command center that turns the live financial store (transactions, bank accounts, loans, invoices, obligations, alerts, firm) into AI-drafted narratives, board materials, scorecards, ratios, and risk/cash briefs. Stakeholders: owner, finance, viewer. Backend: `/api/ai/ask` (POST) for the AI brief/investor update; everything else is computed client-side from the live store, with three tools persisting to KV via `useFeatureState`. Top-level tab array (`CfoView`, 19 entries) drives which component renders.

Helpers shared across tools: `monthBounds(back)` (YYYY-MM-DD month window), `flowsIn(txns,start,end)` (inflow/outflow/net), `pctChange(now,prev)`, `parseBrief(raw)` (markdown → section cards), plus `monthlyBurn`/`runwayDays`/`formatCurrency`/`formatAmount` from `@/lib/utils`.

- **AI Brief** (`ai-brief` → `AiBriefView`) — inputs: live store (transactions, bankAccounts, firm, alerts, activeLoans), an audience toggle (CFO Brief / Investor Update). Computation: builds a deterministic `DATA_BLOCK` (cash balance, runway days, monthly burn, this-vs-last-month revenue + MoM %, expenses, active-loan count/outstanding/EMI, unread-alert count + titles, top-3 vendors by spend, industry), then `buildPrompt()` produces either a CFO-brief prompt (Key Metrics / Revenue Analysis / Cash & Risk Alerts / Top 3 Action Items / Strategic Observations) or an investor-update prompt (Headline / Performance / Cash & Runway / Risks & Mitigations / Asks). POSTs to `/api/ai/ask` with a role-specific system prompt; on success parses the markdown into typed section cards; on failure shows a toast and renders a deterministic fallback brief built from the same real numbers. Output: section cards + 4 always-on quick stats (cash, runway, MoM revenue, active alerts) + a TXT export (`cfo-brief.txt` / `investor-update.txt`). Switching audience clears stale output. _Persist: none (output is in-component state; download only)._ _Class: Backend (AI via `/api/ai/ask`, with Indicative deterministic fallback)._

- **Variance Commentary** (`variance` → `VarianceCommentary`) — inputs: transactions for current vs prior month. Computation: per-category net movement (current − prior), the biggest counterparty swing inside each category as the "why" driver, `pctChange`, and a favourable/unfavourable read (revenue treated inversely to costs); rows sorted by absolute delta. Generates a plain-English sentence per category. Output: variance cards (prev vs cur amounts, delta, %, driver) + a "Copy commentary" clipboard action. _Persist: none._ _Class: Indicative (deterministic, computed from live store)._

- **Board-Deck Generator** (`board-deck` → `BoardDeckGenerator`) — inputs: transactions, bankAccounts, firm, activeLoans, alerts; per-slide include toggles. Computation: assembles 8 text slides from live data — Title, Executive Summary, P&L Snapshot, Cash & Runway, Top Revenue Concentration (top-3 customers w/ % of month), Debt & Obligations, Key Risks (unread alerts), Asks/Next Steps — using current-vs-prior flows, balance, burn, runway, revenue MoM %, total debt/EMI, open risks. Output: toggleable slide cards + "Copy" and "Export Deck" (`board-deck.txt`) actions; only enabled slides export. _Persist: KV key `cfo-deck-slides` (which slides are included) via `useFeatureState`._ _Class: KV (slide selection) + Indicative (slide content)._

- **Risk & Watchlist** (`watchlist` → `RiskWatchlistBrief`) — inputs: transactions, bankAccounts, activeLoans, alerts. Computation: scores up to 5 risk classes — (1) runway <30d critical / <90d high, (2) negative net cash flow (severity scaled vs burn), (3) revenue concentration where top customer ≥40% of month, (4) debt-service load where EMI ≥30% of revenue, (5) open critical/high alerts pulled from the live store — each with severity, heuristic score, and plain-English detail; sorted by score. Output: critical/high/medium count tiles + severity-styled risk cards (or an all-clear state) + "Copy brief". _Persist: none._ _Class: Indicative (heuristic scoring of live signals)._

- **KPI Scorecard** (`scorecard` → `KpiScorecard`) — inputs: transactions, bankAccounts, activeLoans. Computation: 6 KPIs with MoM deltas and a RAG (red/amber/green) status each — Revenue (MoM), Net cash flow, Cash balance (×burn), Runway, Monthly burn (outflow MoM), Debt-service ratio (EMI/revenue) — using current-vs-prior flows, balance, burn, runway, DSCR. Output: KPI cards with RAG dot, value, % delta and hint + "Copy scorecard". _Persist: none._ _Class: Indicative._

- **Cash Snapshot** (`cash-snapshot` → `CashFlowSnapshot`) — inputs: transactions, bankAccounts (current month). Computation: sources-and-uses — groups this month's positive amounts by category (sources) and negative by category (uses), each as a share of its side total; derives opening cash = closing balance − net flow. Output: opening/sources/uses/closing tiles + two share-bar columns (sources vs uses). _Persist: none._ _Class: Indicative._

- **Margin Snapshot** (`margins` → `MarginSnapshot`) — inputs: transactions for current vs prior month. Computation: cash-basis margin — revenue, direct costs (expense + payroll), gross profit/margin, overheads (tax + interest/loan), net profit/margin — for both months with deltas (pp for percentages). Output: gross/net margin tiles + a 7-row line table (prev, cur, Δ with improvement coloring). _Persist: none._ _Class: Indicative (cash-basis approximation, not statutory P&L)._

- **Financial Calendar** (`calendar` → `FinancialCalendar`) — inputs: activeLoans, invoices (current month). Computation: consolidates dated obligations falling in the month — loan EMIs (using nextPaymentDate/amount), unpaid invoices due (receivables), and a fixed GSTR-3B 20th-of-month compliance deadline — sorted by date, flags overdue (date < today). Output: expected-in / committed-out / event-count tiles + a dated event timeline. _Persist: none._ _Class: Indicative (GST date is a fixed assumption)._

- **Top Actions** (`actions` → `TopActionsThisWeek`) — inputs: transactions, bankAccounts, invoices, activeLoans, alerts. Computation: ranks up to 5 highest-impact moves by weight — chase overdue invoices, shore up runway (<90d), reverse negative cash flow, park idle cash (>3-month buffer, with ~6%/yr yield impact estimate), fund EMIs due this month, resolve open critical/high alerts — each carrying a rupee-impact figure; top-5 by weight. Output: numbered check-off action list with impact and completion count. _Persist: KV key `cfo-actions-done` (per-action checked state) via `useFeatureState`._ _Class: KV (check-off state) + Indicative (ranking/impact)._

- **One-Page Summary** (`one-pager` → `OnePageSummary`) — inputs: transactions, bankAccounts, activeLoans, invoices, firm, alerts. Computation: distills the whole position into three blocks — Position (cash, runway, open/overdue AR), Performance MTD (revenue + MoM %, expenses, net flow, burn), Obligations (debt outstanding, EMI/mo, open risks) — plus a rule-based headline takeaway (healthy / caution if runway <45d / stable-but-watch). Output: 3 stat blocks + takeaway card + TXT export (`financial-summary.txt`). _Persist: none (download only)._ _Class: Indicative._

- **Financial Ratios** (`ratios` → `FinancialRatios`) — inputs: transactions, bankAccounts, activeLoans, invoices. Computation: 6 cash-basis ratios each benchmarked good/watch/poor against a healthy band with a plain-English read — Liquidity coverage ((cash+AR)/EMI), Cash-to-burn (months), Debt-to-revenue (vs annualised inflow), Debt-service coverage (revenue/EMI), Receivables intensity (AR/monthly revenue), Expense ratio (outflow/inflow). Output: ratio cards (status dot, value, band, read) + "Copy ratios". _Persist: none._ _Class: Indicative (directional, not statutory)._

- **Profitability Trend** (`trend` → `ProfitabilityTrend`) — inputs: transactions (6-month rolling window). Computation: per-month revenue/expense/net for the last 6 months; counts profitable months, 6-month net trend `pctChange`, and a rule-based verdict (consistently profitable / mixed / mostly loss-making). Output: profitable-months / latest-net / trend tiles + per-month dual mini-bars (revenue vs expense) + verdict card + "Copy trend". _Persist: none._ _Class: Indicative._

- **Expense Control** (`expense-control` → `ExpenseControlScorecard`) — inputs: transactions (current month vs trailing 3-month average). Computation: per expense category, compares this month's spend to the trailing-3-month average baseline, classifies controlled (≤5%) / watch (≤20%) / overrun (>20%); assigns a letter grade A–D by overrun count; sorted by current spend. Output: grade / categories-tracked / overruns tiles + status-styled category cards + "Copy scorecard". _Persist: none._ _Class: Indicative._

- **Loan & Covenant** (`covenant` → `CovenantBrief`) — inputs: activeLoans, transactions. Computation: portfolio DSCR (month revenue / total EMI) plus per-loan repayment progress ((principal−outstanding)/principal) and per-loan DSCR (revenue / loan EMI), flagging a "covenant watch" when DSCR < 1.25×; loans sorted by outstanding. Output: portfolio DSCR / total outstanding / total EMI tiles + per-loan cards (outstanding, EMI, DSCR, next payment, repayment bar, covenant badge) + "Copy brief". _Persist: none._ _Class: Indicative (per-loan DSCR allocates full revenue to each loan)._

- **What Changed** (`what-changed` → `WhatChangedThisWeek`) — inputs: transactions, bankAccounts, activeLoans, invoices; a user-captured baseline snapshot. Computation: builds a current snapshot (cash balance, runway, revenue MTD, net MTD, open AR, overdue AR, total debt) and diffs each metric against the stored baseline, coloring improvements by per-metric good-direction; only changed metrics shown. Output: empty-state / nothing-changed / diff cards (from → to, signed delta) + a "Capture snapshot" / "Re-baseline now" button. _Persist: KV key `cfo-whatchanged-baseline` (the baseline snapshot) via `useFeatureState`._ _Class: KV (baseline) + Indicative (diff)._

- **Liquidity Position** (`liquidity` → `LiquidityPositionBrief`) — inputs: bankAccounts, activeLoans, obligations. Computation: worst-case liquidity test — nets total bank balance against dated obligations + EMIs (×months) falling due in the next 30/60/90 days; per-bucket coverage ratio and ok/shortfall flag (inflows from receivables deliberately excluded). Output: 3 horizon-bucket cards (amount due, covered ×cash or shortfall) + "Copy brief". _Persist: none._ _Class: Indicative._

- **Growth vs Burn** (`growth-burn` → `GrowthVsBurnBrief`) — inputs: transactions for current vs prior month. Computation: burn multiple = cash burned this month ÷ net-new revenue (current − prior inflow); verdict mapped to self-funding / efficient (<1×) / acceptable (1–2×) / expensive (>2×) / burning-no-growth. Output: verdict card + net-new-revenue / net-cash-flow / cash-burned / burn-multiple tiles. _Persist: none._ _Class: Indicative._

- **Top Accounts** (`top-accounts` → `TopAccountsBrief`) — inputs: transactions (current month, by counterparty). Computation: ranks top-5 customers (inflow) and top-5 vendors (outflow) by amount, each as a share of its side total; flags concentration risk (customers) / negotiating leverage (vendors). Output: two ranked share-bar columns + "Copy brief". _Persist: none._ _Class: Indicative._

- **Working Capital** (`working-capital` → `WorkingCapitalBrief`) — inputs: invoices, obligations, activeLoans, transactions. Computation: net working capital = open receivables (with overdue slice) − near-term payables (this-month obligations + one EMI cycle); AR days = open AR ÷ (prior-month revenue / 30). Output: open-receivables / near-term-payables / net-WC / AR-days tiles + a positive/negative headroom narrative card + "Copy brief". _Persist: none._ _Class: Indicative._
