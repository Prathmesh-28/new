## Sustainability & ESG (`/esg`) — 31 tools

Turn live books into an ESG ledger: carbon footprint, Scope 1/2/3, BRSR-lite, green spend, net-zero pathway and India-aware compliance (BRSR, SEBI, CBAM, CPCB EPR, DPDP). Stakeholders: owner, ops. Backend: KV-synced via `useFeatureState` (durable) or transient local state. Emission factors INDICATIVE — grid 0.71 kg CO₂e/kWh (CEA), diesel 2.68, petrol 2.31, LPG 2.98; spend-based EEIO factors per ₹1,000.

The tab bar has 32 entries; **Overview** is a dashboard, not a tool, so 31 tools below.

- **Overview** (dashboard, not counted) — reads live `store.transactions` → revenue (Σ positive), expense (Σ |negative|), rough screening footprint (expense/1000 × 35 mixed factor), framework badges; jump-buttons to 10 tools. _Persist: none (live store)._ _Class: Backend._
- **Carbon Footprint Estimator** (`footprint`) — annual ₹ per 6 spend categories (fuel 70, electricity 55, travel 40, logistics 50, materials 30, services 8 kg/₹1,000) → kg = (₹/1000)×factor per row → total footprint, categorised spend, largest source, % breakdown bars; shows live annual expense as hint. _Persist: none (transient useState)._ _Class: Indicative (live-store hint)._
- **Scope 1/2/3 Calculator** (`scopes`) — Scope 1: diesel/petrol litres + LPG kg × fuel factors; Scope 2: kWh × 0.71; Scope 3: purchased/logistics/travel ₹ × spend proxies (30/50/40 per ₹1,000) → per-scope tCO₂e, total inventory, % split bar (GHG Protocol). _Persist: none (transient)._ _Class: Indicative._
- **Energy & Water Tracker** (`energy`) — monthly log: month, kWh, water kL, bill ₹; add/remove rows → totals (kWh, Scope 2 = kWh×0.71, water kL, spend) + per-row CO₂e table sorted by month. _Persist: `esg-utility-log`._ _Class: KV._
- **ESG Scorecard** (`scorecard`) — 12 yes/no checkboxes across E/S/G (4 each) → per-pillar % (yes/total), overall = mean of 3 pillar %, band (Leader ≥75 / Progressing ≥50 / Developing ≥25 / Starting out). _Persist: `esg-scorecard`._ _Class: KV._
- **BRSR-Lite Readiness** (`brsr`) — 12 checklist items in 4 sections (General/Environment/Social/Governance) → completed count + % progress bar; 100% banner. _Persist: `esg-brsr-lite`._ _Class: KV._
- **Green Spend Tracker** (`greenspend`) — description + ₹ amount + type (7 green types) → total green spend, % of live annual expense, count + line-item table. _Persist: `esg-green-spend`._ _Class: KV (live-store ratio)._
- **Emission Intensity** (`intensity`) — total tCO₂e + revenue (₹, auto-fills from live revenue) + optional prior-year intensity → carbon intensity = tonnes ÷ (rev/1cr), per ₹lakh variant, YoY % delta with improving/worsening verdict. _Persist: none (transient)._ _Class: Indicative (live-store auto-fill)._
- **Supplier Sustainability Rating** (`supplier`) — name + 3 sliders (emissions/labour/governance 1–5) + ISO-14001 certified flag → score = min(100, (sum/15)×100×(certified?1.1:1)), tier (Low ≥75 / Watch ≥50 / High risk) table sorted by score. _Persist: `esg-suppliers`._ _Class: KV._
- **Carbon Offset Cost Estimator** (`offset`) — residual tCO₂e + base ₹/t (default 1200) + quality tier (basic ×0.5 / verified ×1 / removal ×3) → effective unit price, tonnes, total offset cost. _Persist: none (transient)._ _Class: Indicative._
- **Sustainability Goal Tracker** (`goals`) — goal name, unit, baseline/current/target, target year → SBTi-style progress = (baseline−current)/(baseline−target) clamped 0–100, achieved banner. _Persist: `esg-goals`._ _Class: KV._
- **Employee Commute Emissions** (`commute`) — per-leg: mode (6 factors, pax-km), # employees, one-way km → annual kg = emp×km×2×22 days×12×factor (Scope 3 cat 7) → total footprint, employees covered, per-employee + leg table. _Persist: `esg-commute`._ _Class: KV._
- **Business Travel Emissions** (`travel`) — short-haul (0.158) + long-haul (0.195) pax-km, rail (0.041), road (0.171) km, hotel nights (12 kg/night) → per-mode tCO₂e (Scope 3 cat 6), total, stacked split bar. _Persist: none (transient)._ _Class: Indicative._
- **Waste & Recycling Tracker** (`waste`) — per-stream (7 streams): generated kg, recycled kg (validated ≤ generated) → totals, diversion rate = recycled/generated, to-landfill, per-row diversion table (BRSR/EPR metric). _Persist: `esg-waste`._ _Class: KV._
- **Renewable Switch ROI (Rooftop Solar)** (`renewable`) — monthly grid kWh, tariff ₹/kWh (8), system kW, capex ₹/kW (55000) → capex, annual gen (kW×1460), usable gen capped at consumption, bill saving, simple payback, CO₂ avoided (×0.71), 25-yr net benefit; <6yr payback banner. _Persist: none (transient)._ _Class: Indicative._
- **EV-Fleet Transition Calculator** (`evfleet`) — vehicles, km/yr (15000), ICE mileage (15 km/L), fuel ₹/L (100), EV km/kWh (7), charge ₹/kWh (9) → ICE vs EV running cost & emissions (petrol 2.31 vs grid 0.71), cost & CO₂ savings, narrative. _Persist: none (transient)._ _Class: Indicative._
- **Diversity & Social Metrics** (`diversity`) — 8 headcount inputs (total, women, mgmt total/women, PwD, contractual, training hrs, safety incidents) → women %, women-in-mgmt %, PwD %, contractual %, training hrs/emp, incidents (BRSR Section C, P3/5). _Persist: `esg-diversity`._ _Class: KV._
- **Governance Checklist** (`governance`) — 12 items in 4 groups (Board/Ethics/Risk/Data, Companies Act + DPDP 2023) → completed count + % bar. _Persist: `esg-governance`._ _Class: KV._
- **CBAM Export-Exposure Estimator** (`cbam`) — per-line: product (5 goods, embedded-emission intensities tCO₂e/t: steel 2.1, aluminium 8.6, cement 0.87, fertiliser 1.6, hydrogen 10) + tonnes to EU; CO₂ price €/t (75) + €→₹ (90) → embedded emissions, ₹/t price, annual CBAM liability + line table. _Persist: `esg-cbam`._ _Class: KV (Indicative factors)._
- **EPR Compliance Tracker** (`epr`) — per-category (5 CPCB categories): obligation kg, fulfilled kg, cert ₹/kg (15) → totals, compliance % = fulfilled/obligation, shortfall, cost-to-close = shortfall×cert price, met/short status table. _Persist: `esg-epr`._ _Class: KV._
- **Green-Loan Eligibility Score** (`greenloan`) — 8 weighted criteria checkboxes (15/15/10/15/20/10/10/5 = 100) + optional loan ₹ → score, band (Strong ≥75 / Eligible ≥50 / Not ready), indicative rate step-down (0.75%/0.25%/0%), est. annual interest saving = loan×stepDown. _Persist: `esg-greenloan`._ _Class: KV (Indicative rates)._
- **Net-Zero Pathway Planner** (`netzero`) — baseline tCO₂e, base year, net-zero year, residual % (10) → linear glide path: per-year cap = base−(base−residual)×frac, required annual cut, annual cut %, SBTi-aligned check (≥4.2%/yr), residual, years-to-net-zero, sampled cap-by-year bars. _Persist: none (transient)._ _Class: Indicative._
- **Supplier ESG Questionnaire Tracker** (`questionnaire`) — add supplier; advance through 4-stage flow (not_sent→sent→responded→verified) → suppliers tracked, sent/awaiting, verified, response rate = (responded+verified)/total + status table with Advance action. _Persist: `esg-supplier-survey`._ _Class: KV._
- **ESG Report Builder** (`report`) — business name, year, select from 8 report sections → exports plain-text outline (.txt download via Blob) of chosen sections. _Persist: `esg-report-sections` (section selection)._ _Class: KV (export Simulated — .txt skeleton, not PDF)._
- **Climate-Risk Assessment** (`climaterisk`) — 8 risks (4 Physical + 4 Transition) rated 0–3 sliders → per-type % = sum/(items×3), overall = mean, band (High ≥60 / Moderate ≥30 / Low) — TCFD-style screen. _Persist: `esg-climate-risk`._ _Class: KV._
- **Sustainable Procurement Scorecard** (`procurement`) — per-category: total spend ₹, sustainable spend ₹ (validated ≤ total) → totals, green-procurement share = sustainable/total, per-row share table. _Persist: `esg-procurement`._ _Class: KV._
- **Community / CSR Impact Tracker** (`csr`) — per-project: project, theme (6), spend ₹, beneficiaries → total spend, people reached, cost/beneficiary, project count; shows indicative 2% of live profit (Sec 135) as hint (BRSR P8). _Persist: `esg-csr`._ _Class: KV (live-store profit hint)._
- **Energy Cost-Savings Tracker** (`energysavings`) — per-measure: measure, capex ₹, annual ₹ saving, CO₂e cut t/yr → total capex, annual saving, blended payback = capex/saving, total CO₂ cut; table ranked by payback (fastest first). _Persist: `esg-energy-savings`._ _Class: KV._
- **Emissions per Employee** (`empintensity`) — total tCO₂e, FTE headcount, optional prior-year per-head → per-employee tCO₂e (and kg), YoY % delta with improving/worsening verdict. _Persist: none (transient)._ _Class: Indicative._
- **Renewable vs Grid Mix** (`renewmix`) — grid / on-site solar / REC kWh → renewable share = (solar+rec)/total, total consumption, grid emissions = grid×0.71, emissions avoided = renewable×0.71, energy-mix split bar (RE100-style). _Persist: none (transient)._ _Class: Indicative._
- **Green Certification Checklist** (`certs`) — 8 certifications in 4 groups (Environment/Product/Social/Governance: ISO 14001/50001/45001/27001, IGBC/GRIHA, BIS Ecomark, EPR CPCB, SA8000); click to cycle Not started→In progress→Certified → held + in-progress counts. _Persist: `esg-certifications`._ _Class: KV._
- **ESG Rating Improvement Planner** (`ratingplan`) — 10 weighted actions across E/S/G (points 6–12, effort Low/Medium/High) checkboxes → projected rating = earned/max ×100, band (Strong ≥75 / Moderate ≥50 / Emerging), points earned, actions done/open, top-3 quick wins (low-effort, high-point first). _Persist: `esg-rating-plan`._ _Class: KV._
