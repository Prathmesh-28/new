## Benchmarks (`/benchmarks`) — 21 tools

Benchmarks your live financials against your own trailing-12-month quartiles and indicative sector reference bands. Stakeholders: owner, all. Backend: KV-synced (reads `transactions`, `bankAccounts`, `invoices`, `inventory`, `procurement`, `activeLoans`, `fixedAssets`, `firm`; the only writable input is headcount, persisted via `useFeatureState` → `store.featureData`). NOTE: sector reference bands are INDICATIVE — directional guides for typical Indian SMBs, not live peer data (every tool re-states this). Sector selector (10 sectors) drives bands; data-backed bands exist only for `default`, `Manufacturing (SMB)` and `IT Services`, all others fall back to `default`.

Tabs (tool selector array): Overview, Industry Ratios, Cost Structure, Growth Percentile, Working-Capital, Profitability, Expense Ratios, Per-Employee, Digital Score, Valuation Multiple, Stock Turn, Tax Burden, EBITDA Margin, Revenue Stability, Liquidity Buffer, Debt Leverage, Opex Efficiency, Net Margin Band, Cash Runway Band, Customer Retention. (20 tabs + the Overview default = 21 distinct tools.)

- **Overview (Peer Scorecard)** — current-month revenue, costs (ex-payroll), payroll, monthly burn, bank balance + selected sector → computes gross margin, cash runway (days), revenue growth (MoM), payroll/revenue, burn multiple; ranks each into a percentile (90/65/40/20) and, where ≥3 months of history exist, replaces gross-margin/payroll/growth bands with the tenant's OWN trailing-12-month P25/P50/P75 (via `percentiles`); renders radar (you vs median), a circular composite peer score, per-metric quartile labels, and a P25/median/P75 bar breakdown with a "gap-to-median" coaching tip per metric → overall quartile position + per-metric ranking. _Persist: live store (read-only)._ _Class: Indicative (self-quartile bands are Backend-derived; sector fallback is Indicative)._
- **Industry Ratio Benchmarking** — TTM revenue/cost/payroll, bank balance, open invoices, active-loan EMIs → current ratio ((balance+openAR)÷(3×monthly EMI)), net margin (after est. interest = 40% of EMI), opex/revenue, interest coverage (EBIT÷est. interest), revenue/cash-assets; each placed on a sector low/mid/high band via linear `bandPercentile` → composite percentile, TTM revenue, TTM net profit, count benchmarked, and a per-ratio table with position bar. _Persist: live store (read-only)._ _Class: Indicative (live-computed ratios vs indicative bands; interest/liability proxies disclosed)._
- **Peer Cost-Structure Benchmark** — TTM revenue, cost, payroll vs sector cost-mix reference (payroll/COGS/rent/marketing/admin %) → live payroll/revenue and operating-spend/revenue ratios (non-payroll collapsed into one line since txns aren't sub-categorised) compared to peer payroll % and summed peer non-payroll %, plus a peer reference cost-mix stacked bar with legend; flags lines above peer benchmark (pp gap) → your structure vs peers. _Persist: live store (read-only)._ _Class: Indicative._
- **Growth-Rate Percentile** — trailing-12-month monthly revenue series → compound monthly growth rate (`cmgr`, needs ≥2 positive months) and latest MoM growth; ranks CMGR within a sector growth distribution (P10/P25/P50/P75/P90, piecewise-linear interpolation) → your CMGR, latest MoM, segment percentile + a distribution bar chart highlighting bars at/below your CMGR. _Persist: live store (read-only)._ _Class: Indicative._
- **Working-Capital Benchmark (CCC)** — invoices, inventory, procurement, transactions → DSO (`dso`), DIO (`dio`), DPO (`dpo`); CCC = DIO+DSO−DPO vs sector norm CCC; computes gap-days × average daily spend run-rate (annual spend ÷ active months ÷ 30) for cash locked/freed; per-component bars with norm markers and improvement hints → your CCC, sector norm, gap, cash impact. _Persist: live store (read-only)._ _Class: Indicative (CCC live-computed via 90-day proxies; sector norms indicative)._
- **Profitability Percentile** — TTM revenue and direct cost → gross margin %; ranks within sector gross-margin distribution (P10–P90, piecewise-linear) and computes rupee upside of reaching the sector-median margin (gap% × TTM revenue) → gross margin, sector percentile, upside-to-median + margin distribution bar chart. _Persist: live store (read-only)._ _Class: Indicative._
- **Expense-Ratio Benchmark** — TTM revenue + per-category transaction sums (operating spend, payroll, tax) + active-loan annual interest run-rate (outstanding × rate) → each line as % of TTM revenue vs sector low/mid/high bands (lower is better for all); counts lines above median and sums rupee spend above median lines → per-line `BandRow` comparison. _Persist: live store (read-only)._ _Class: Indicative._
- **Productivity (Revenue / Employee)** — TTM revenue, cost, payroll + user-entered headcount → revenue/employee (₹ lakh p.a.), profit/employee (revenue−cost−payroll ÷ headcount), avg payroll/head; revenue-per-employee placed on sector RPE band → per-head output cards + `BandRow` vs sector. _Persist: KV — headcount saved via `useFeatureState("bmk-headcount")` → `store.featureData`._ _Class: Indicative (headcount input is KV-persisted; bands indicative)._
- **Digital-Maturity Scorecard** — counts of bank accounts, transactions, invoices, inventory SKUs, purchase orders, fixed assets, GST-registered flag, % spend categorised → weighted score (8 checks summing to 100; e.g. bank 18, txn 16, invoicing 16, GST 14, inventory 12, procurement 10, assets 8, categorised 6); maps to tier (Digital leader/Digitising well/Early adopter/Mostly manual) and surfaces best next step → circular score, capability checklist with per-check detail and points. _Persist: live store (read-only)._ _Class: Backend (scored entirely from own usage signals, no peer data; weights indicative)._
- **Valuation-Multiple Benchmark** — TTM revenue, cost, payroll → EBITDA margin; quality factor (margin/25, clamped 0–1) nudges applied EV/Revenue multiple within sector low–high band; computes low/median/high/your-estimate EV (revenue × multiple) → TTM revenue, EBITDA margin, applied multiple, indicative EV + valuation-range bar chart. _Persist: live store (read-only)._ _Class: Indicative (revenue-multiple estimate only; multiples indicative)._
- **Stock-Turn Benchmark** — inventory value (qty×unitCost) + 90-day received-PO COGS annualised (×4) → inventory turns/year (annualCogs÷invValue) vs sector turn norms; flags slow/dead stock (SKUs at/below reorder level holding value) → turns, sector median, inventory value, dead-stock value/count + `BandRow`. _Persist: live store (read-only)._ _Class: Indicative (turns live-computed via 90-day proxy; norms indicative)._
- **Tax-Burden Benchmark** — TTM net GST (`gstSummary` summed across months at firm `gstRate`, default 18%, after input credit) + direct tax cash paid (tax-category outflows) → total tax burden as % of TTM revenue vs sector band; splits GST %-of-rev and direct %-of-rev → burden, net GST, direct tax, sector median + `BandRow`; warns on above-range burden (ITC leakage). _Persist: live store (read-only)._ _Class: Indicative (GST is an estimate not a filed return; bands indicative)._
- **EBITDA-Margin Benchmark** — TTM revenue, cost, payroll → EBITDA (revenue−cost−payroll) and EBITDA margin % vs sector band; rupee upside to sector-median margin (gap% × revenue) → margin, EBITDA, sector median, upside + `BandRow`; warns when below band low. _Persist: live store (read-only)._ _Class: Indicative (pre-interest/tax/depreciation proxy; bands indicative)._
- **Revenue-Stability Benchmark** — trailing-12-month positive-revenue months → mean, std deviation, coefficient of variation (std÷mean ×100, needs ≥3 months); CV placed on sector CV band (lower is steadier) → volatility, avg monthly revenue, std deviation, sector median CV + monthly-revenue spread bar chart (bars vs mean) and `BandRow`. _Persist: live store (read-only)._ _Class: Indicative._
- **Liquidity-Buffer Benchmark** — total bank balance ÷ monthly burn (`monthlyBurn`) → months of opex held in cash vs sector buffer band; computes shortfall cash to reach sector-median buffer (band.mid × burn − balance) → months of buffer, cash balance, monthly burn, to-median gap + `BandRow`; warns below band floor. _Persist: live store (read-only)._ _Class: Indicative._
- **Debt-Leverage Benchmark** — active-loan outstanding principal and annual EMI vs TTM revenue → debt/revenue multiple (lower safer) placed on sector leverage band, plus debt-service % of revenue (annual EMI÷revenue) → debt/revenue, outstanding debt + loan count, debt-service ratio, sector median + `BandRow`; warns above band low; notes "unlevered" when no loans. _Persist: live store (read-only)._ _Class: Indicative._
- **Opex-Efficiency Benchmark** — TTM revenue and operating cost (ex-payroll) → opex/revenue % vs sector opex band (lower is leaner); rupee saving from trimming to sector median (gap% × revenue) → opex ratio, operating spend, sector median, saving + `BandRow`; warns above band low. _Persist: live store (read-only)._ _Class: Indicative._
- **Net-Margin Band** — TTM revenue, cost, payroll less estimated loan interest (~40% of annual EMI) → net profit and net margin % vs sector net-margin band; rupee upside to sector median → net margin, TTM net profit, sector median, upside + `BandRow`; warns below band low. _Persist: live store (read-only)._ _Class: Indicative (pre-tax operating net margin; interest estimated; bands indicative)._
- **Cash-Runway Band** — total bank balance ÷ monthly burn (`monthlyBurn`) → months of net burn covered vs sector runway band; cash needed to reach sector-median runway ((band.mid − months) × burn) → runway, cash balance, monthly burn, cash-to-median + `BandRow`; warns below band low. _Persist: live store (read-only)._ _Class: Indicative._
- **Customer-Retention Band** — invoices split into prior (>6 months ago) vs recent (last 6 months) customer sets by case-insensitive name → repeat rate = retained (prior customers re-invoiced recently) ÷ prior customers, vs sector retention band → retention %, prior customers, retained, lapsed counts + `BandRow`; warns below band low with win-back nudge. _Persist: live store (read-only)._ _Class: Indicative (retention live-computed from invoices; name-matching caveat; bands indicative)._
