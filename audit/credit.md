## Credit & Embedded Lending (`/credit`) — 30 tools

Embedded-lending and credit-intelligence workspace: real backend underwriting + offer acceptance plus a deep suite of indicative loan-sizing, eligibility, cost-comparison and score-planning calculators for Indian SMEs. Stakeholders: owner, finance. Backend: `/api/credit` (apply / accept / payment) + KV-synced (`store.featureData` via `useFeatureState` → localStorage + backend, plus `creditApplications`/`creditOffers`/`activeLoans` in the synced AppContext store).

### Tab-selector array (30 tabs, in order)

`overview` · `apply` · `loans` · `notyet` · `wc` · `equip` · `cc` · `fd` · `wcscore` · `captable` · `valuation` · `aapull` · `matcher` · `comscore` · `discount` · `docpack` · `foir` · `emicalc` · `flatred` · `dscr` · `drawing` · `gstelig` · `lap` · `prepay` · `odterm` · `scoreplan` · `offercmp` · `invadv` · `nbfcbank` · `scheme`

> Shared helpers: `emi(P, annualRate%, months)` = standard reducing-balance EMI; `totalInterest()` = EMI×n − P. A proactive amber CTA fires when runway is 1–44 days. The page header carries a `PreviewBadge` for the `creditDisbursement` capability (real disbursement is gated/preview).

### Core lending flow (backend-backed)

- **Overview** (`overview`) — what it does: renders the application/offer dashboard. Inputs → live `creditApplications`, `creditOffers`, `activeLoans`, `transactions`, `bankAccounts` from the synced store → outputs three KPIs (best UW score `Math.max` of application scores, max approved amount, active-loan count); the **Pre-Qualified Offers** grid (real server offers for the approved application, recomputing EMI/total interest/total repayment client-side via `emi()`, "Best offer" = largest amount, with an RBI-Digital-Lending APR/cooling-off note); a **"Right time to borrow?"** verdict (≥65 = borrow now; else wait-30-days if this-month revenue > 1.05× last-month, else points-to-50 message); an **EMI capacity gauge** (sum of active-loan EMIs as % of monthly burn, safe/caution/high-risk bands at 25%/40%, and headroom `(burn×0.3 − emiLoad)/1000` ₹K/mo); and a static **score-factors weighting** table (9 signals: revenue consistency 25, avg revenue 20, business age 15, debt-service 15, customer concentration 10, overdraft 8, engagement 7). _Persist: live store (read-only)._ _Class: Backend (offers/score) + Indicative (timing/capacity computed client-side)._

- **Apply** (`apply`) — what it does: submit a pre-qualification. Inputs → loan amount (₹, min 100000), term (6/12/18/24/36 mo), purpose (7 options) → `POST /api/credit/apply {requested_amount, term_months, purpose}` returns `{application, offers[], underwriting:{score, approved_amount}}`; persists a `creditApplication` (status approved if offers exist, else rejected) and each real `creditOffer` keyed by its server offer id (rate normalised `apr_equivalent×1000/10`). Honest error handling: 429 → "one application every 90 days", 409 → "in progress", else underwriting-unreachable toast (never fabricates approval). Also shows a static **hardship-protection** list (revenue drop >30% → 60-day pause, etc.). _Persist: `creditApplications` + `creditOffers` (live store) + backend._ _Class: Backend._

- **Active Loans** (`loans`) — what it does: track disbursed loans. Inputs → `activeLoans` store list → per loan shows outstanding/principal, % repaid progress bar, next-payment date+amount; **Record Payment** posts `POST /api/credit/loans/:id/payment {amount}` then decrements outstanding and rolls next-payment date +1 month; **Early repayment calculator** (slider 1→termMonths) recomputes EMI on outstanding and shows interest saved = `totalInterest(outstanding,rate,termMonths) − totalInterest(outstanding,rate,customMonths)`. Loan creation happens via the KFS-modal **Accept** flow → `POST /api/credit/accept/:offerId` → adds an `ActiveLoan`, marks application `funded`. _Persist: `activeLoans` (live store) + backend payment._ _Class: Backend._

- **Not yet** (`notyet`) — what it does: explains the score gap for declined/low-score users. Inputs → 6-month monthly revenue (computes CoV = std/mean), business age (months since first transaction), per-account overdraft proxy (days cumulative balance < 0), top-counterparty concentration (top payer ÷ 6-month revenue), avg monthly revenue → outputs a checklist of 5 improvements each with a point value (CoV>0.25 +8, age tiers +10/+5, concentration>40% +6, overdrafts +5, revenue<₹3L +5), projected score = `min(100, bestScore + gapTotal)`, and an indicative limit `meanRev×3`. _Persist: none (live store read; checklist derived, not saved)._ _Class: Indicative (computed from live transaction data)._

- **KFS modal** (rendered, not a tab) — what it does: RBI Digital Lending 2022 Key Facts Statement before accepting an offer; shows lender, amount, APR, term, EMI, total interest/repayment, ₹999 processing fee, prepayment/cooling-off/grievance terms; "Accept Loan" triggers the backend accept flow. _Persist: triggers backend `accept` + `activeLoans`._ _Class: Backend._

### Working-capital, treasury & cap-table tools

- **WC Sizing** (`wc`, `WCSizingTab`) — what it does: Tandon Committee 2nd-method working-capital limit. Inputs → annual net sales (auto-seeded from sum of positive transactions), debtor days / inventory days / creditor days sliders → daily sales = rev/365; AR req = dailySales×debtorDays, inventory req = dailySales×invDays×0.6 (COGS≈60%), AP credit = dailySales×0.6×creditorDays, net WC = AR+inv−AP → output MPBF = `0.75 × net WC requirement` (indicative limit). _Persist: none (form-local; annual-rev pre-seeded from store)._ _Class: Indicative._

- **Finance vs Lease** (`equip`, `EquipmentFinanceLease`) — what it does: after-tax total-cost-of-ownership compare for buying (term finance) vs operating lease of an asset. Inputs → asset cost, down-payment %, loan rate/tenure, monthly lease rent/tenure, residual buyout %, corporate tax % → finance: EMI on (cost−DP), gross = DP+EMI×n, tax saving = 15% WDV depreciation × tax × years; lease: gross = rent×months + residual, tax saving = full rent deductible × tax × years → outputs net-of-tax cost for each and the cheaper "winner" with the delta. _Persist: none (form-local)._ _Class: Indicative._

- **CC Utilization** (`cc`, `CcUtilizationTab`) — what it does: track business credit cards and utilisation. Inputs → per-card name/bank/limit/balance/due-date/min-due (min-due auto = 5% of balance) → outputs totals (limit, balance, overall utilisation %, total min due), >70% utilisation warning, per-card util bar (green/orange/red at 30%/70%) and overdue-flagging; balance is editable inline. _Persist: KV — `useFeatureState("credit-cards")`._ _Class: KV._

- **FD / RD** (`fd`, `FdRdTab`) — what it does: fixed/recurring-deposit maturity tracker. Inputs → kind (FD/RD), bank, principal/total, monthly instalment (RD), rate, tenure+unit, start date, TDS-applicable flag → FD interest = quarterly compounding `P((1+r/4)^(months/3)−1)`; RD interest via the standard RD maturity formula; TDS = 10% of interest if flagged; computes maturity date, net interest, days-left, maturing-in-30-days, maturity value → portfolio KPIs + sorted cards with elapsed-time progress bars. _Persist: KV — `useFeatureState("fd-rd")`._ _Class: KV._

- **WC Health Score** (`wcscore`, `WcHealthScore`) — what it does: weighted working-capital health grade. Inputs → current assets/liabilities, inventory, receivables, payables, annual revenue (auto-estimated from store) and COGS (default rev×0.6) → computes current ratio, quick ratio, DSO, DPO, DIO, cash-conversion-cycle (DSO+DIO−DPO), working capital → scores 5 ratios on good/bad thresholds (weights 25/20/20/15/20, DSO & DIO inverted) → weighted /100 score with Excellent/Good/Fair/Poor grade + per-component bars. _Persist: none (form-local; revenue seeded from store)._ _Class: Indicative._

- **Cap Table** (`captable`, `CapTableTab`) — what it does: equity ownership register + dilution simulator. Inputs → shareholders (name, share class of 6, shares held, amount invested; seeded with founders/ESOP/angel) → outputs total shares, total capital, founder ownership %, sorted ownership bars; **Dilution simulator** inputs new investment + pre-money → post-money = pre+invest, new investor % = invest/post, price/share = pre÷existing shares, new shares = invest/price, and a before/after dilution table per holder. _Persist: KV — `useFeatureState("cap-table")` (holders); simulator inputs form-local._ _Class: KV (register) + Simulated (dilution)._

- **Valuation** (`valuation`, `ValuationTab`) — what it does: three-method enterprise-value estimator + blend. Inputs → DCF (annual FCF, growth %, WACC %, terminal growth %, projection years) → year-by-year projected FCF discounted to PV + discounted terminal value `FCFₙ(1+g)/(WACC−g)` (guarded: WACC must exceed terminal growth); revenue-multiple (annual revenue auto-annualised from store × multiple); EBITDA-multiple (EBITDA × multiple) → outputs each EV plus blended = equal-weight average of methods with a value. _Persist: none (form-local; revenue auto-seeded)._ _Class: Indicative._

### Underwriting & eligibility analysers

- **AA Underwriting** (`aapull`, `AaUnderwritingPull`) — what it does: models the credit profile a lender derives from an Account Aggregator bank-statement feed (no live AA fetch). Inputs → avg monthly inflow + avg balance (seeded from live transactions/bank balances), existing EMI outflow, banking vintage, inflow CoV, top-payer concentration, bounces (6mo), overdraft days (6mo) → 7 weighted sub-scores (inflow 25, stability 20, buffer 15, bounces 15, OD days 10, vintage 10, concentration 5) → /100 score + risk band (Prime/Near-prime/Sub-prime/Decline-likely); indicative limit = 50% FOIR on surplus (inflow−EMI), capitalised at ~16% over 36 mo, then risk-adjusted ×1/0.7/0.4/0 by band. _Persist: none (form-local; inflow/balance seeded from store)._ _Class: Indicative (estimator — explicitly no AA backend call)._

- **Eligibility Matcher** (`matcher`, `LoanEligibilityMatcher`) — what it does: matches profile against 6 hardcoded Indian SME products. Inputs → annual turnover, vintage (mo), bureau score, collateral-available flag → each product checks 4 gates (min turnover/vintage/score, collateral need); match % = passed/4, indicative ticket = `min(maxTicket, 25% of turnover)` shown only if fully eligible → sorted by odds then rate. Products: Unsecured Term Loan, WC OD/CC, CGTMSE, Invoice/Bill Discounting, LAP, Merchant Cash Advance. _Persist: none (form-local)._ _Class: Indicative._

- **Commercial Score** (`comscore`, `CommercialScoreTracker`) — what it does: logs business bureau scores over time. Inputs → bureau (CIBIL MSME Rank 1–10 / CRIF Highmark / Experian), score, as-of date, note → validates CMR 1–10, tracks latest reading, change-vs-prior (inverts "improving" direction for rank where lower is better), risk/quality verdict bands, full reverse-chronological table with delete. _Persist: KV — `useFeatureState("commercial-credit-score")`._ _Class: KV._

- **Invoice Discounting** (`discount`, `InvoiceDiscountingConnector`) — what it does: TReDS-style receivable listing + financing-bid estimator. Inputs → buyer/drawee, invoice no, amount, due date (→ tenure days), discount rate % p.a. → discount charge = `face × rate × tenure/365`, advance ≈ 90% of face, net now = advance−charge, effective annualised cost; listed↔funded status toggle → portfolio totals. _Persist: KV — `useFeatureState("invoice-discounting")`._ _Class: KV (listings) + Indicative (bid math)._

- **Loan Doc Pack** (`docpack`, `LoanDocumentPack`) — what it does: auto-assembles the lender document checklist. Inputs → loan type (unsecured/WC/LAP/CGTMSE) + entity (proprietorship/partnership-LLP/Pvt Ltd) → filters ~16 documents by applicability rules into KYC/Financial/Collateral groups, ticking persists, shows %-ready progress and a "ready to submit" banner at 100%. _Persist: KV — `useFeatureState("loan-doc-pack")` (checked map); selectors form-local._ _Class: KV._

### Loan-math calculators

- **FOIR / Capacity** (`foir`, `FoirCalculator`) — what it does: Fixed-Obligation-to-Income-Ratio repayment-capacity test. Inputs → net monthly income (seeded = (revenue−expenses)/months from store), existing EMIs (seeded from active loans), other fixed obligations, FOIR ceiling slider 30–70%, proposed new rate/tenure → current FOIR = obligations/income, EMI headroom = `income×cap − obligations`, max new principal reverse-solved from the EMI formula at the proposed rate/tenure; verdict bands at cap and 0.8×cap. _Persist: none (form-local; income/EMI seeded)._ _Class: Indicative._

- **EMI & Schedule** (`emicalc`, `EmiAmortizationTab`) — what it does: reducing-balance EMI with full amortization table. Inputs → principal, rate % p.a., tenure (1–360 mo) → EMI via `emi()`, total interest, total repayment, interest/principal ratio, and month-by-month principal/interest/balance split (final EMI clears rounding residue). _Persist: none (form-local)._ _Class: Indicative._

- **Flat vs Reducing** (`flatred`, `FlatVsReducingTab`) — what it does: exposes the true cost of a flat-rate quote. Inputs → principal, quoted rate %, tenure → flat interest = `P×rate×years`, flat EMI = (P+flatInterest)/months; binary-searches the reducing-balance rate that yields the same EMI (the effective rate); compares against a genuine reducing loan at the same nominal rate and shows the extra cost. _Persist: none (form-local)._ _Class: Indicative._

- **DSCR** (`dscr`, `DscrCalculator`) — what it does: Debt-Service-Coverage-Ratio covenant check. Inputs → annual NOI (seeded ≈ annualised revenue−expenses from store), existing annual debt service (seeded = active-loan EMI×12), new loan amount/rate/tenure → current DSCR = NOI/existingDebt, post-loan DSCR = NOI/(existing+new annual debt), max new loan keeping DSCR≥1.25 (reverse EMI solve); verdict bands at 1.5/1.25/1.0 + debt-service build-up table. _Persist: none (form-local; NOI/debt seeded)._ _Class: Indicative._

- **Drawing Power** (`drawing`, `DrawingPowerTab`) — what it does: OD/CC drawing-power from stock & debtor statement. Inputs → closing stock, stock margin %, creditors-against-stock, eligible debtors, debtor margin %, optional sanctioned limit + currently utilised → paid stock = stock−creditors, DP = `paidStock×(1−stockMargin) + debtors×(1−debtorMargin)`, effective limit = min(sanction, DP), available/utilisation %, over-drawn warning. _Persist: none (form-local)._ _Class: Indicative._

- **GST Eligibility** (`gstelig`, `GstEligibilityTab`) — what it does: GST-turnover loan-limit estimator. Inputs → annual GST turnover, net margin %, sales multiple ×, consecutive GSTR filings → turnover method = monthly sales × multiple; profit-coverage method = 50% of annual profit serviceable, capitalised 36 mo @18%; filing factor (≥12mo=1, ≥6=0.7, ≥3=0.4, else 0.15) → indicative limit = `min(turnover, profit) × filingFactor`. _Persist: none (form-local)._ _Class: Indicative._

- **LAP / LTV** (`lap`, `LapLtvTab`) — what it does: Loan-Against-Property eligibility. Inputs → property market value, existing loan on property, property type (residential 70 / commercial 60 / industrial 50% base LTV), occupancy (self 0 / rented −5 / vacant −10 adj), rate, tenure → applicable LTV (min 30%), gross eligible = value×LTV, net = gross−existing, EMI + total interest on net via `emi()`. _Persist: none (form-local)._ _Class: Indicative._

- **Prepayment Optimizer** (`prepay`, `PrepaymentOptimizer`) — what it does: compares the two prepayment outcomes. Inputs → outstanding/rate/months-left (quick-fillable from an active loan), lump-sum, prepay/foreclosure fee % → Option A (keep EMI, shorten tenure): new tenure via `−ln(1−P·r/EMI)/ln(1+r)`, months saved, interest saved; Option B (keep tenure, lower EMI): new EMI on reduced principal, EMI reduction, interest saved; each saving nets the fee, highlights the larger. _Persist: none (form-local; pre-fill reads active loans)._ _Class: Indicative._

- **OD vs Term Loan** (`odterm`, `OdVsTermLoanTab`) — what it does: true-cost compare for fluctuating WC needs. Inputs → amount/limit, period (mo), OD rate, TL rate, avg OD utilisation %, OD setup fee %, TL processing fee % → OD cost = `avgUtilised × odRate × months/12 + setupFee` (interest only on used balance); TL cost = total reducing-balance interest + processing fee → cheaper winner + delta. _Persist: none (form-local)._ _Class: Indicative._

### Planning & comparison tools

- **Score Planner** (`scoreplan`, `ScoreImprovementPlanner`) — what it does: commit to score-improving actions and project the gain. Inputs → base score (max of real application scores) + 6 toggleable actions with point values (consistency +8, buffer +5, diversify +6, GST filing +7, reduce EMI load +6, age tier +5) and effort tags → projected = `min(100, base + gained)`, points-left, progress bar to the 50 approval line. _Persist: KV — `useFeatureState("cr-scoreplan-done")` (durable committed-actions map)._ _Class: KV (plan) + Indicative (projection anchored to real score)._

- **Compare 3 Offers** (`offercmp`, `ThreeOfferCompare`) — what it does: side-by-side effective-APR compare of 3 competing offers. Inputs per offer → lender, amount, rate, months, fee %, insurance % → EMI + total interest via helpers; fee/insurance as ₹ of amount; true total cost = interest+fee+insurance; effective APR = `(totalCost/amount)/(months/12)`; flags the lowest true-total-cost "Best". _Persist: KV — `useFeatureState("cr-offercmp")` (3 offer rows)._ _Class: KV (inputs) + Indicative (math)._

- **Invoice Advance** (`invadv`, `InvoiceAdvanceCalculator`) — what it does: cash-today-vs-cost on a single unpaid invoice. Inputs → invoice face value, advance % (50–95), processing fee %, financing rate % p.a., expected days to payment → advance = face×advance%, reserve = face−advance, finance cost = `advance×rate×days/365`, process fee = face×fee%, net upfront = advance−costs, final net once buyer pays = netUpfront+reserve, effective annual cost = `(totalCost/advance)/(days/365)`. _Persist: none (form-local)._ _Class: Indicative._

- **NBFC vs Bank** (`nbfcbank`, `NbfcVsBankCompare`) — what it does: prices rate vs speed across a bank and an NBFC. Inputs → amount, tenure, cost-of-waiting (₹/day), and per-channel rate/fee/disbursal-days → for each: EMI, total reducing-balance interest, fee ₹, delay cost = days×₹/day, true total = interest+fee+delayCost → cheaper winner + delta (only meaningful once cost-of-delay > 0). _Persist: none (form-local)._ _Class: Indicative._

- **Scheme Finder** (`scheme`, `SchemeFinder`) — what it does: filters 7 Indian MSME credit/subsidy schemes by profile. Inputs → amount needed, purpose, women-owned / new-business / collateral-free flags → matches schemes where need≤max, purpose included, and women/new/collateral filters satisfied → sorted ascending by limit, each with max, eligibility text and tags. Schemes: MUDRA Shishu/Kishor/Tarun, CGTMSE, Stand-Up India, PMEGP, PSB Loans in 59 Minutes. _Persist: none (form-local; scheme catalog hardcoded)._ _Class: Indicative._

### Persistence & classification summary

- **Backend (`/api/credit`):** Apply, Active Loans (record payment), KFS-modal Accept flow, and the Overview offer/score cards (server-returned offers and underwriting score).
- **KV-synced (`useFeatureState` → `store.featureData`):** CC Utilization (`credit-cards`), FD/RD (`fd-rd`), Cap Table holders (`cap-table`), Commercial Score (`commercial-credit-score`), Invoice Discounting (`invoice-discounting`), Loan Doc Pack (`loan-doc-pack`), Score Planner (`cr-scoreplan-done`), Compare 3 Offers (`cr-offercmp`).
- **Indicative / Simulated (form-local, no persistence):** WC Sizing, Finance vs Lease, WC Health Score, Valuation, AA Underwriting (explicitly no live AA call), Eligibility Matcher, FOIR, EMI & Schedule, Flat vs Reducing, DSCR, Drawing Power, GST Eligibility, LAP/LTV, Prepayment Optimizer, OD vs Term Loan, Invoice Advance, NBFC vs Bank, Scheme Finder, plus the Not-yet gap analyser and the Cap Table dilution simulator. Several seed their inputs from live store data (transactions, bank balances, active loans) but do not save their own state.
