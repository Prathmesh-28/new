# Headroom — The Complete Finance Operating System for Indian SMBs

**One app to run the entire money side of your business** — billing, GST, payroll, compliance, cash flow, credit, and an AI CFO — built India-first for the business owner and a small team (you, your finance person, your CA, sales, and ops).

> **1,400+ working tools · 55 modules · Web · iOS · Android · WhatsApp-native**

---

## Why Headroom

Running a small business in India means juggling a dozen tools and still flying blind:

- **Cash flow is the #1 killer** — late customer payments, surprise expenses, seasonal swings. Most owners are "profitable but broke."
- **GST & compliance never stop** — e-invoicing, ITC mismatches, TDS, ROC, PF/ESI, notices, penalties. One missed date = ₹25k + interest.
- **You can't afford a CFO** — so no one is watching runway, margins, or risk until it's too late.
- **The ₹20–25 lakh-crore credit gap** — only ~14% of India's 64M MSMEs get formal credit, because lenders can't see your real cash flows.
- **Everything lives in different places** — Tally, spreadsheets, WhatsApp, the bank portal, your CA's email.

**Headroom replaces that mess with one intelligent system.** Tally, Zoho, Vyapar, ClearTax, RazorpayX — each does a slice. Headroom does the whole picture, with an AI CFO on top and WhatsApp built in.
