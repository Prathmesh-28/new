## Predict & Digital Twin (`/predict`) — 27 tools

A model-based digital twin that war-games decisions, stress-tests cash and surfaces risk from the firm's own ledger/invoices. Stakeholders: owner, finance. Backend: KV-synced (every tool reads the live `useApp().store` — transactions, invoices, bankAccounts, activeLoans, firm — via `useTwinMetrics`; nothing writes except Scenario Compare). All figures are framed as estimates, not guarantees.

The `TABS` array (27 entries) drives the tab bar; each renders one component. `useTwinMetrics` is the shared engine: it buckets `store.transactions` into a contiguous trailing 12-month series, derives avg monthly revenue/expense/net (over active months), total revenue/expense, net cash (rev−exp as runway proxy), runway months, revenue volatility (coefficient of variation = std-dev/mean), and outstanding A/R (unpaid invoices).

- **Overview** (`OverviewTab`) — live store → 4 KPI cards (avg monthly revenue, avg monthly net, est. cash runway, revenue volatility %), a 12-month revenue-vs-expense area chart (the "training window"), and a clickable launcher grid jumping to every other tab; empty-state if no history. _Persist: none._ _Class: Indicative._
- **Digital Twin** (`DigitalTwin`) — live store → 8 consolidated tiles (avg monthly revenue/expense/net, net cash generated, runway, outstanding A/R, revenue volatility %, overdue-invoice count) + monthly net-cashflow bar chart (green/red per sign). Mirrors the present, no projection. _Persist: live store._ _Class: Backend (pure read-out of live data)._
- **What-If Sliders** (`WhatIfSliders`) — revenue Δ%, cost Δ%, horizon(3–36mo) sliders → applies flat % shifts to monthly rev/cost, linear projection of cumulative profit (net×month) base vs scenario → 4 KPIs (new monthly rev/cost/net, cumulative lift) + dual-line cumulative-profit chart. _Persist: none (local useState)._ _Class: Simulated (what-if)._
- **Monte-Carlo Cash** (`MonteCarloCash`) — opening cash, runs(500–10k), horizon(3–18mo) → Box-Muller normal-shock simulation: each run compounds monthly net ± random normal (sd anchored to revenue volatility) over horizon, counts ruin (cash<0) → P10/P50/P90 ending cash, cash-out risk %, and a P10/P50/P90 fan area-chart. Uses `Math.random` inside the run handler only. _Persist: none._ _Class: Simulated (Monte-Carlo)._
- **Scenario Compare** (`ScenarioCompare`) — add/remove scenarios (name, rev Δ%, cost Δ%) seeded with Optimistic/Base/Recession → each applies % shifts to run-rate → table of projected monthly net & annual net (×12). _Persist: KV key `pred-scenarios` via `useFeatureState`._ _Class: KV (Simulated compute on stored inputs)._
- **Early Warning** (`EarlyWarningBoard`) — live store → rules-based traffic-light board of 6 signals (cash runway, monthly profitability, revenue volatility, overdue receivables, customer concentration = top customer % of invoiced value, data freshness = days since last txn) each classed critical/high/medium/ok by thresholds. _Persist: none._ _Class: Indicative (rules-based early-warning)._
- **Trend Projection** (`TrendProjection`) — project-ahead(1–12mo) slider + seasonal toggle → OLS linear regression of revenue on month index, optional per-month seasonal index (month/overall-mean) scaling → trend direction (rising/falling ₹/mo), projected next-3-months sum, actual-vs-projected line chart. _Persist: none._ _Class: Indicative (linear+seasonal trend)._
- **Churn Risk** (`ChurnRisk`) — invoices grouped per customer → heuristic churn score = clamp(days-since-last-invoice/90 ×0.6 + overdue-ratio ×0.4)×100 → High/Medium/Low flag, ranked table (last invoice, count, billed, overdue, score bar). _Persist: none._ _Class: Indicative (heuristic churn)._
- **Break-Even** (`BreakEvenSimulator`) — monthly fixed cost (pre-filled ~60% of avg expense), price/unit, variable cost/unit → contribution margin (P−V), break-even units (F÷contribution) & revenue, current units (run-rate rev÷price), covered? verdict. _Persist: none._ _Class: Simulated (classic break-even)._
- **Sensitivity** (`SensitivityTornado`) — driver-swing(5–50%) slider → flexes Revenue (+) and Expense (−) by ±swing% around run-rate, measures swing in monthly net → tornado bar chart (downside/upside) sorted by impact + per-driver ± sensitivity. _Persist: none._ _Class: Simulated (sensitivity/tornado)._
- **Goal Probability** (`GoalProbability`) — cash target, deadline(1–24mo), revenue-stress-drop% → applies revenue drop then runs 3,000 Box-Muller randomized cash paths from current cash → probability of hitting target, median projected cash, stressed monthly net. _Persist: none._ _Class: Simulated (stress-test + Monte-Carlo goal prob)._
- **Run-Rate** (`RunRateProjector`) — base-window(1–12mo) + assumed monthly growth% sliders → avg recent monthly revenue → flat ARR (×12) and compounded 12-month projection at growth rate → 4 KPIs + projected monthly-revenue bar chart. _Persist: none._ _Class: Simulated (run-rate annualisation)._
- **Expense Creep** (`ExpenseCreepDetector`) — expense txns by category → compares last-3-month avg vs prior-3-month avg per category → change %, flags >15% "Creeping" / <−15% "Falling" / else "Stable" + KPI summary + table. _Persist: none._ _Class: Indicative (period-over-period spend drift)._
- **Customer LTV** (`CustomerLtv`) — expected-lifespan(6–120mo) + gross-margin% sliders → per customer: monthly billing (total÷tenure in months) × lifespan × margin → predicted LTV ranking + totals. _Persist: none._ _Class: Indicative (LTV estimate)._
- **Pay-Delay** (`PaymentDelayPredictor`) — invoices per customer → predicted days-to-pay from invoice→due term (paid) plus overdue lateness (overdue), averaged → Slow/Moderate/Prompt habit + open overdue/amount table. _Persist: none._ _Class: Indicative (payment-behaviour estimate; explicitly proxies lateness as it's "unknowable")._
- **Cohort Retention** (`CohortRetention`) — invoices → first-invoice month = acquisition cohort, active-if-billed-that-month → last 6 cohorts × 6 month-offsets retention % heatmap (color-graded M0–M5). _Persist: none._ _Class: Indicative (heuristic cohort retention)._
- **Working Capital** (`WorkingCapitalForecast`) — DSO + DPO sliders → receivables tied up (dailyRev×DSO), payables float (dailyExp×DPO), net working capital (AR−AP), cash freeable from AR (openAR − arNeed) + open-vs-target bar. _Persist: none._ _Class: Simulated (working-capital model)._
- **Revenue Band** (`RevenueConfidenceBand`) — project-ahead(1–12mo) slider → OLS trend midpoint with band widening by sd×√(k+1)×1.28 (≈P10/P90) anchored to revenue volatility → P10/P50/P90 area+line forecast band + next-month KPIs. _Persist: none._ _Class: Indicative (probabilistic revenue band)._
- **Seasonality** (`SeasonalityForecast`) — revenue series → seasonal index per month (month/12-mo-mean ×100, 100=average) → peak/trough/spread KPIs + index bar chart vs 100 line. _Persist: none._ _Class: Indicative (seasonal decomposition)._
- **Concentration** (`ConcentrationRisk`) — invoices by customer → revenue share %, Herfindahl-Hirschman Index (Σpct²), top-1/top-3 share → risk verdict (High/Moderate/Diversified) + top-8 share bar chart. _Persist: none._ _Class: Indicative (concentration risk / HHI)._
- **Recurring Spend** (`RecurringSpendForecast`) — expense txns by counterparty → flagged `isRecurring` OR repeated 3+ times treated as subscription → avg charge annualised (×12) → recurring-vendor count, projected annual/monthly cost, largest, table (Flagged/Inferred source). _Persist: none._ _Class: Indicative (subscription-sprawl projection)._
- **Cash-Out Day** (`CashBalanceProjection`) — opening cash (pre-filled from `bankAccounts` balances or net cash) + project-ahead(3–36mo) → adds avg monthly net per month, names first month balance turns negative → opening/net/lowest-balance/cash-out-month KPIs + balance area chart with 0 reference line. _Persist: none._ _Class: Indicative (linear cash-balance projection)._
- **Invoice Pay-Date** (`InvoicePayDatePredictor`) — open invoices → learns each customer's typical invoice→due term from history (portfolio median fallback), adds to invoice date → predicted settlement date, days-out, confidence (High ≥3 samples / Medium ≥1 / Low), expected-within-30d totals + sorted table. _Persist: none._ _Class: Indicative (per-invoice pay-date prediction)._
- **GST Liability** (`GstLiabilityForecast`) — output-rate (pre-filled from `firm.gstRate`) + ITC% sliders → output tax (rev×rate) − input credit (×ITC%) = net GST/mo, ×3 quarter reserve, per-month net-GST bar; warns if `firm.gstRegistered` is false. _Persist: none (reads firm settings)._ _Class: Indicative (GST-liability forecast)._
- **Payroll Stress** (`PayrollStressPredictor`) — opening cash (from bank balances), monthly payroll (pre-filled from last-3-month `payroll`-category txns), min buffer → projects cash after each salary run over 12 mo, flags first month breaching buffer → payroll runs covered, first-breach month + after-payroll bar chart vs buffer line. _Persist: none._ _Class: Indicative (payroll cash-stress)._
- **Debt Service** (`DebtServiceProjection`) — `activeLoans` + extra-principal% slider → aggregates outstanding/EMI/outstanding-weighted avg rate, amortises combined balance monthly (cap 60mo) with optional extra principal → payoff month, total interest, outstanding-balance area chart. _Persist: none (reads activeLoans)._ _Class: Indicative (loan amortisation projection)._
- **Profit Trajectory** (`ProfitTrajectory`) — project-ahead(3–36mo) + margin-drift(±3 pts/mo) sliders → base net margin drifted per month, applied to revenue run-rate → monthly net + cumulative profit, current/end margin, trajectory verdict (Expanding/Compressing/Flat) + dual-line chart. _Persist: none._ _Class: Simulated (margin-drift profit projection)._
