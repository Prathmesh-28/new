## Income Tax & TDS (`/tax`) — 38 tools

"Tax Autopilot" — a single-page Income-Tax/TDS workbench that turns the firm's live P&L into advance-tax, TDS, capital-gains, regime, presumptive, corporate and compliance estimates plus durable trackers (TCS/TDS-return/26AS/197/notices/MSME). Stakeholders: owner, finance, CA. Backend: mixed — calculator tools are client-side `useState` (nothing persisted); record-keeping trackers use `useFeatureState` (KV-synced to `store.featureData`, debounced to backend, propagated across devices); the Overview reads the live `store.transactions` and writes to the live Forecast store via `addObligation`.

### Overview dashboard (`taxTab === "overview"`) — composite live tool

The default tab. Reads live `store.transactions` and `firm`. YTD window = `startOfYear(today)` to today (`amount > 0` = revenue, `amount < 0` = expense). Five sub-widgets:

- **Summary cards (4)** — inputs: live txns + `firm.gstRate`/`gstRegistered`. Compute: YTD Net Profit (rev − exp); Annual Tax Estimate = `ytdProfit × 0.25` (flat 25% effective rate, not slab-based); GST This Month = last-month revenue × `gstRate%` (default 18%) if GST-registered; Next Deadline = days to nearest auto-computed deadline (red ≤10d, yellow ≤30d). _Persist: live store (read-only)._ _Class: Indicative._
- **YTD P&L Snapshot bar** — Revenue / Expenses / Profit as % of revenue bars. _Persist: live store._ _Class: Backend (reads synced txns)._
- **TDS Deposit quick card** — TDS estimate = current-month expense txns (`category === "expense"`) × 2% flat; labels "7th of next month." Heuristic 2%, not section-accurate. _Persist: live store._ _Class: Indicative._
- **Upcoming Deadlines timeline** — `computeTaxCalendar(today)` (`TaxPage.tsx:27-64`) generates up to 10 sorted deadlines: Advance Tax (15 Jun 15% / 15 Sep 45% / 15 Dec 75% / 15 Mar 100%), GSTR-3B (20th, next 4 months), TDS Deposit (7th, next 3 months), ITR Filing (31 Jul). Each row shows estimated amount (advance-tax instalment via `installmentDue` map = annualTaxEst × 15/45/75/100%; GSTR-3B = gstLiability; TDS = tdsEst) and an "Add to Forecast" button → `addObligation({type:"tax"})` then navigates to `/forecast`. _Persist: live store; obligations persist to the live Forecast/store._ _Class: Backend._
- **GST Filing CTA + empty-state** — navigates to `/gst` or `/transactions`. _Persist: none._ _Class: Preview._

### Calculator & tracker tools

- **Regime Optimizer (`regime`)** — `RegimeOptimizer` (`TaxPage.tsx:1045`). Inputs: gross income, 80C (cap ₹1.5L), 80D (₹25k / ₹50k senior), 80CCD(1B) NPS (cap ₹50k), 24(b) home-loan interest (cap ₹2L), HRA exemption, senior-citizen flag. Old regime: gross − ₹50k − Chapter VI-A/24(b) deductions; slabs 0/5/20/30% at 2.5/5/10L (`OLD_BANDS`); 87A rebate = min(slab, ₹12,500) if taxable ≤ ₹5L; +4% cess. ### Accuracy notes for auditors

- **Overview Annual Tax Estimate and TDS card are flat-rate heuristics** (25% effective, 2% TDS), not slab/section computations — explicitly "Indicative."
- **Angel Tax surcharge thresholds in code** (>₹1cr 12%, >₹10L 7%) differ from the standard individual 50L/1cr trigger; verify before relying.
- **234B/234C and Interest 234 tool** use proportional/assumed-month approximations (each labels itself indicative).
- **Equalisation Levy 2% (e-commerce)** is retained though withdrawn w.e.f. 1-Aug-2024 — kept for prior-period entries (stated in-tool).
- KV-persisted trackers (TCS, TDS Return, 26AS, 197, Depreciation, Loss Set-off, Eq-Levy, Tax Notice, 80G donations+AGTI, 43B(h)) survive reload and sync across devices; all other calculators reset on navigation.
