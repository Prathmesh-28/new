## Analytics & BI (`/analytics`) — 35 tools

Self-serve financial analytics and BI workbench that turns the live transaction/invoice/order/asset store into P&L, cash-flow, profitability, concentration, forecasting and predictive views. Stakeholders: owner, finance, sales. Backend: KV-synced (every tab reads the live `useApp()` store — transactions, invoices, inventory, fixedAssets, activeLoans, bankAccounts, orders, firm); tool-specific assumptions persist via `useFeatureState` (KV). Page-level PDF/Excel export covers Monthly P&L + top customers/vendors.

- **Overview** — live transactions → revenue (sum +amt)/expense (|−amt|)/net/margin per month over 3/6/12-mo range; Revenue-vs-Expense bar/area chart (toggle series), Net-margin line, expense category pie (transfers excluded), top-5 revenue customers & top-5 expense vendors by counterparty share → dashboard. _Persist: none (range/chartType are local UI state)._ _Class: KV._
- **Revenue** — +amt transactions → this-month vs prev-month revenue, avg monthly (÷6), revenue concentration (top customer %), monthly revenue bar trend, per-customer revenue table with share bars → revenue dashboard. _Persist: live store._ _Class: KV._
- **Expenses** — −amt transactions → expense-by-category horizontal bar (last 30 days), top vendors by spend table (with % and inferred category), top-3 category cards with % of total expense. _Persist: live store._ _Class: KV._
- **Benchmarks** — your ratios (Payroll/Rev, OpEx/Rev, Net margin, Tax/Rev) vs static reference figures (35/40/15/8%) + efficiency ratios (Rev/Employee placeholder, Gross margin, Burn multiple, Operating leverage) → comparison bars. Reference points are hardcoded, explicitly "not live peer data." _Persist: live store._ _Class: Indicative._
- **P&L Deep Dive** — category totals → income-statement bridge (Revenue → −OpEx → Gross Profit → −Payroll −Taxes → EBIT → +Depreciation(est. 1.5% of rev) → EBITDA → −Interest(est. 35% of loan pmts) → Net Income), unit-economics cards (annual run-rate, avg ticket, EBITDA/gross margin, rev-per-₹-expense, burn multiple), monthly income-statement table (direct costs est. 50% of expense), revenue-quality (recurring %, customer concentration, HHI). _Persist: live store._ _Class: Indicative (depreciation/interest/direct-cost are estimates)._
- **Cash Flow** — all transactions bucketed into 12 months → inflows/outflows/net/cumulative position, signed net-flow bar + cumulative line, inflow-vs-outflow bars, monthly table, CSV export. Independent of range selector. _Persist: live store._ _Class: KV._
- **Concentration** — revenue-by-customer & spend-by-vendor by counterparty → top-1/top-3 share, HHI (Herfindahl, Σshare²×10000), risk banner (>40% high / >20% medium), ranked bars; lender-scrutiny threshold at 20%. _Persist: live store._ _Class: KV._
- **Sales vs Target** — owner-entered monthly targets (global apply or per-row) vs actual revenue → variance, achievement %, on-track/near-miss/below-target badges, totals. _Persist: `sales-targets` (KV)._ _Class: KV._
- **Cash Forecast** — trailing 6-mo avg revenue/expense × user sliders (forecast months 1-12, monthly growth %, cost change %, opening cash) → projected revenue/expense/net + cumulative cash, shortfall warning. _Persist: none (slider state local)._ _Class: Simulated (forward projection)._
- **Balance Sheet** — auto-derived assets (bank balances, unpaid invoices=AR, inventory qty×unitCost, fixed-asset net book value per Schedule II depreciation) + manual inputs (other current, AP, short borrowings, taxes payable, other long-term, share capital, fixed override) → retained earnings as balancing figure, current ratio, balanced check. _Persist: none (manual inputs local state)._ _Class: Indicative (management-use, not statutory Schedule III)._
- **Ratio Analysis** — annualised revenue/net-profit/EBIT from trailing-12mo tagged transactions + store-prefilled manual balance-sheet inputs → liquidity (current, quick), profitability (net margin, gross margin, ROE, ROA), leverage (D/E lowerBetter, interest coverage), efficiency (asset turnover, inventory turnover) with good/watch/poor grades vs indicative SME benchmarks. _Persist: none (manual inputs local)._ _Class: Indicative._
- **Trial Balance** — tagged transactions → cash-basis ledger (Sales credit, OpEx/Salaries/Taxes debit, Loan dr/cr by sign, Transfers, Cash & Bank as contra balancing account so dr=cr) → debit/credit columns, balanced check, CSV export. _Persist: live store._ _Class: Indicative (simplified cash-basis, not full double-entry)._
- **Sales Commission** — tiered (marginal/slab) or flat-% structure on manually-added salespeople's sales → per-person commission, effective rate, total payout, avg rate; TDS 194H/GST note. _Persist: `commission-mode`, `commission-flat-rate`, `commission-tiers`, `commission-people` (KV)._ _Class: KV._
- **SKU Profitability** — items inferred from transaction descriptions (2-word title-case proxy) × assumed COGS% (default 60%, per-item overridable) → revenue, units, gross margin, margin%, winners (≥40%) vs losers (<20%), top-8 margin bar. _Persist: `sku-default-cogs`, `sku-cogs-overrides` (KV)._ _Class: Indicative (no SKU master; COGS assumed)._
- **Customer Cohorts** — revenue by counterparty → first/last seen, months active, monthly rev, LTV proxy (monthlyRev×24×0.5 contribution), active(≤90d)/churned status, acquisition cohorts by first-seen month with retention %, per-customer profitability table. _Persist: live store._ _Class: Indicative (LTV/churn heuristic)._
- **Region P&L** — segment transactions by real bank account OR derived "region" (deterministic hash of counterparty into N/S/E/W/Central — no geo field) → per-segment revenue/expense/net/margin, best/weakest, grouped bar. _Persist: `branch-segment-by` (KV)._ _Class: Indicative (region is a proxy bucket)._
- **Unit Economics** — assumptions (S&M % of expense=15, gross margin %=50, lifetime months=24) → CAC (S&M spend ÷ new customers), LTV (monthly ARPA×lifetime×GM), LTV:CAC ratio (target ≥3x), CAC payback months, net-LTV bridge. _Persist: `ue-sm-pct`, `ue-gm-pct`, `ue-lifetime` (KV)._ _Class: Indicative (assumption-driven)._
- **Sales Funnel** — owner-entered leads/qualified + invoices (issued=proposals, paid=won; fallback to revenue-txn count) → 4-stage funnel with stage conversion %, overall win rate (won÷leads), won value, open invoices. _Persist: `funnel-leads`, `funnel-qualified` (KV)._ _Class: Indicative (no CRM; proxied from invoices)._
- **Expense Variance** — 12-mo monthly expense → MoM or YoY* mode, this-month vs base variance (abs & %), per-category cost-driver variance table (current vs prev/~12mo-ago), 12-mo trend line. YoY proxied to earliest month if <12mo history. _Persist: `variance-mode` (KV)._ _Class: KV._
- **Pareto 80/20** — revenue by customer OR item (deriveItem) → ranked contribution with cumulative %, count driving 80% of revenue, top-entity share, HHI, Pareto composed chart (bars + cumulative line), highlighted 80% cut-off row. _Persist: `pareto-dim` (KV)._ _Class: KV (item dim is Indicative — inferred)._
- **Margin Bridge** — base revenue × what-if levers (price %, volume %, unit-cost %, base COGS %=60) → price/volume/cost profit effects, new gross profit & margin, profit-impact, waterfall bars. Price→profit, volume→contribution at margin, cost→COGS. _Persist: `bridge-price`, `bridge-volume`, `bridge-cost`, `bridge-cogs-pct` (KV)._ _Class: Simulated (what-if model)._
- **Churn Flags** — heuristic scoring: customer churn risk from revenue recency/frequency/cadence (>90d=+40, ≤1 txn=+25, overdue vs own cadence) + invoice late-payment risk (overdue status, days overdue, prior-overdue same customer) → High/Med/Low lists, revenue-at-risk, exposure-at-risk. _Persist: live store._ _Class: Indicative (heuristic, explicitly not ML)._
- **Margin Trends** — trailing-12mo revenue/expense × assumed COGS% (default 60%) → monthly gross margin % vs net margin %, avg gross/net, this-month net, net-margin trend (pts vs prev), dual line chart. _Persist: `anl-mt-cogs-pct` (KV)._ _Class: Indicative (gross uses assumed COGS)._
- **Expense Ratios** — trailing-12mo expense by category (Operating/Payroll/Taxes/Loan) as % of monthly revenue → 12-mo avg per category cards, stacked area chart, total tracked-cost ratio. _Persist: live store._ _Class: KV._
- **AR Ageing** — open (unpaid) invoices bucketed by days overdue from due date (0-30/31-60/61-90/90+) → total receivables, overdue >30d, 90+ provisioning candidate, DSO (AR ÷ avg daily revenue over trailing 6mo), bucket bars + pie + open-invoice detail table. _Persist: live store._ _Class: KV._
- **Break-Even** — trailing-6mo revenue/cost → payroll + assumed fixed-share% (default 55) of non-payroll = fixed cost; contribution-margin ratio; break-even revenue (fixed÷CM ratio); margin of safety; actual-vs-break-even line chart. _Persist: `anl-be-fixed-pct` (KV)._ _Class: Indicative (fixed/variable split assumed)._
- **Working Capital** — per-month (12mo): DSO (open AR ÷ daily revenue run-rate), DPO (~30-day purchase proxy), assumed DIO (default 30), CCC = DSO+DIO−DPO → KPI cards + trend lines. _Persist: `anl-wc-inv-days` (KV)._ _Class: Indicative (DPO/DIO proxied)._
- **Seasonality** — revenue transactions → day-of-week heat grid (intensity vs busiest day), monthly seasonality index (100=avg active month), peak day/month, weekend share. _Persist: live store._ _Class: KV._
- **Refund Impact** — negative-revenue txns + outflows matching refund/return/credit-note/discount/rebate regex → 12-mo gross vs net revenue, refund total, leakage rate %, avg/month, stacked gross-net + leakage-% chart, largest-refunds table. _Persist: live store._ _Class: KV._
- **Per-Employee** — annualised (trailing-12mo) revenue/profit/payroll ÷ owner-entered headcount → revenue/employee, profit/employee, payroll/employee, revenue-per-₹-payroll multiple, vs owner-set benchmark (default ₹15L). _Persist: `anl-pe-headcount`, `anl-pe-benchmark` (KV)._ _Class: Indicative (no HR module; headcount/benchmark entered)._
- **YoY Growth** — revenue per month vs same calendar month one year prior (subYears) over 3/6/12-mo window → this-period vs prior-year revenue, absolute & % growth, this-year-vs-last-year bars, monthly growth decomposition table. Needs 13+ months history. _Persist: `anl-yoy-window` (KV)._ _Class: KV._
- **New vs Repeat** — first-ever revenue date per counterparty (full history) defines new vs repeat within lookback window → new/repeat revenue & customer counts, retention/acquisition mix %, pie split. _Persist: `anl-nvr-months` (KV)._ _Class: KV._
- **Weekday Pattern** — revenue transactions grouped by booking day-of-week → strongest/weakest day, weekend share, revenue-by-day bar (peak highlighted), per-day table (revenue, count, avg ticket, share). _Persist: live store._ _Class: KV._
- **AOV Trend** — revenue ÷ revenue-transaction count per month over 6/12-mo → overall AOV, latest-month AOV, peak month, MoM % change, composed AOV-line + transaction-count-bar chart. _Persist: `anl-aov-range` (KV)._ _Class: KV._
- **Channel Split** — `orders` store grouped by source (WhatsApp/Email/Excel/Manual/Phone), cancelled excluded → total order value, top channel, channel concentration %, pie mix + per-channel table (value, count, avg order, share). _Persist: live store (orders)._ _Class: KV._
