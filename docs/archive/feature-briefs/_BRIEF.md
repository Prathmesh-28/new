# Research Brief — Headroom "2090" Feature Catalog

**Mission:** Build a forward-looking ("year 2090") catalog of features for **Headroom**, an India-first SMB/SME finance super-app. Visionary and decades ahead, but every feature must be a **concrete, describable capability** (never vague). Ground each feature in ONE of: a **competitor gap**, a real **SMB/SME pain point**, or an **emerging/future fintech trend** (below).

## Competitors to beat
- **India accounting/GST:** Tally Prime (on-prem, inventory depth, the CA default), Zoho Books (cloud automation), Vyapar / myBillBook (mobile-first billing), ClearTax (GST filing/ITC), Busy, GimBooks, HostBooks, Saral, Munim, Giddh, Refrens.
- **India fintech:** RazorpayX (+ Payroll), Open / Zwitch, Khatabook, OkCredit, Pice, Cashflo, KredX, Recur Club, CredAble, Decentro.
- **Global:** QuickBooks, Xero, NetSuite, Sage; Brex, Ramp, Mercury, Rho, Airbase (spend/banking — now shipping AI agents: Brex AI answers natural-language spend questions; Ramp "Stack" is an AI finance OS).

## SMB/SME pain points (researched, 2025–26)
- **Cash flow is the #1 killer:** unexpected expenses (42%), late customer payments (35%), seasonal swings (29%); revenue tied up in credit; "profitable but no cash."
- **Credit gap:** ₹20–25 trillion (~$530B) MSME credit gap; only ~14% of 64M MSMEs have formal credit; collateral-based lending excludes them.
- **GST burden:** e-invoicing 30-day reporting rule + ₹5cr threshold sweeping in MSMEs; ITC mismatches (GSTR-2B vs books; vendors don't upload); DRC-01 / ASMT-10 notices; ₹25k-per-error penalties + 24% interest; GSTR-3B can't be revised.
- **Compliance overload** across GST/TDS/ROC/PF/ESI/PT/labour/licenses; owners have no CFO; manual reconciliation; fragmented tools; no single source of truth.

## Future / frontier trends (express through a 2090 lens)
- **Agentic AI:** autonomous agents executing multi-step finance workflows; ~80% of transactions AI-driven by 2030; AI-CFO co-pilots; agent-to-agent negotiation.
- **Embedded finance:** lending / insurance / payroll / wealth woven into workflows; just-in-time working capital at point of need.
- **Real-time & ambient:** instant payments, Request-to-Pay, event-driven money, "invisible"/background finance.
- **India DPI 2.0:** Account Aggregator + OCEN (cash-flow-based lending), UPI credit lines, ONDC commerce, CBDC/e-rupee programmable money, Aadhaar + DEPA consent.
- **Tokenization & programmable money,** smart-contract settlement & escrow; **quantum** risk/fraud modeling; **cross-border**/GIFT-City; **ESG/carbon** accounting; **data sovereignty/privacy** (DPDP).

## 2090 framing
Assume: ubiquitous autonomous AI finance agents; ambient/invisible finance; programmable money; real-time everything; neural / AR / spatial interfaces; self-driving books; a predictive **digital twin** of the business; zero-touch compliance; agent-to-agent commerce. Be bold but always specific — describe the capability, who it serves, and the pain/gap/trend it answers.
