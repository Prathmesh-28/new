# Headroom — The 2090 Catalog: 5,600 Features

A web-researched, competitor-grounded, pain-driven vision of where an India-first SMB/SME finance super-app goes over the next several decades. **40 themes × 140 features = 5,600**, each one a concrete capability tagged with the stakeholder it serves and the competitor gap / SMB pain / future trend it answers.

Full lists live in [`features-2090/`](features-2090/) (one file per theme). Research grounding in [`features-2090/_BRIEF.md`](features-2090/_BRIEF.md).

---

## How this was built
1. **Web research** across competitors, SMB/SME pain points, and where fintech is heading (sources below).
2. **40 themes** spanning the entire SMB-finance universe, from today's table-stakes to a bold "year 2090" frontier.
3. **140 features per theme**, each escalating from practical → visionary (the back third of every file is deliberately decades-ahead: autonomous agents, ambient/invisible finance, programmable money, business digital-twins, zero-touch compliance).

## What the web research found (grounding)
- **Competitors to beat** — India: Tally Prime, Zoho Books, Vyapar/myBillBook, ClearTax, Busy, GimBooks, Refrens, RazorpayX (+Payroll), Khatabook, Cashflo, KredX, Recur Club. Global: QuickBooks, Xero, NetSuite, Brex, Ramp, Mercury, Rho — the leaders are now shipping **AI finance agents** (Brex AI, Ramp "Stack").
- **Sharpest SMB pains** — cash flow is the #1 killer (unexpected expenses 42%, late payments 35%, seasonality 29%); a **₹20–25 trillion (~$530B)** MSME credit gap with only ~14% of 64M MSMEs formally financed; GST overload (e-invoicing 30-day rule, ITC/GSTR-2B mismatches, DRC notices, ₹25k/error penalties, no GSTR-3B revision); fragmented tools and no CFO.
- **Where it's heading** — agentic AI (~80% of transactions AI-driven by 2030), embedded finance, real-time/ambient money, India DPI 2.0 (Account Aggregator + OCEN, UPI credit lines, ONDC, CBDC/e-rupee, DEPA consent), tokenization & programmable money, quantum risk, invisible finance by 2040.
